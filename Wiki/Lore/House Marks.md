A secret way of indicating what the contents of a building contains.

Some known ones that can be found on the Scratch Wall.

- XXO: No stag-turners (safe house)
- OXO: Blood with muscle (freelance thug)
- OXOXO: No peelers (shady gambling den)
- OOXX: Cool jink for hot junk (a fence)
- XOOX: If you ain't ready for the dead-book (healer)
- XXOO: Bye-bye, berk (abondoned building with a portal)
