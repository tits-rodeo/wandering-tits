##### NPC Name
> *Species, hair, clothes, skin*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Easy to remember way to roleplay the character. When generating randomly, make this a direct reference to an existing character in popular media.
- **Voice:** Voice to use.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** Some way to make the character interesting.
- **Home:** Where they're from/live.
- **Purpose:** Why they're here at the current location.
- **Attitude Toward Party:** Loves, Friendly, Mildly Optimistic Toward, Mildy Suspicous Of, Dislikes, or Hates. There is no Neutral. Also include reasoning for their attitude.
