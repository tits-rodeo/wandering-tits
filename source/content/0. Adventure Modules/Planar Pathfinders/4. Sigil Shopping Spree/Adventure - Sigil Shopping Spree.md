# Sigil Shopping Spree

## Session Summary

A single-session adventure set in Sigil's Market Ward. The session covers Mina's departure from the party, Corvus's introduction as a new companion, a shopping spree, and the discovery of Boss's sentient warehouse.

### Expected Chronology

1. **The Burnt Brick** — Party witnesses Corvus mid-brawl, then incapacitated by a thrown chair. His metal tetsubo is dented. Mina offers to repair it at her grandfather's smithy.
2. **Fork Off** — Neither Mina nor Corvus know where Bashers for Bashers is. Corvus suggests asking Boss. Boss gives the smithy's location for free. For any other information, his price is finding and inspecting his missing warehouse.
3. **Bashers for Bashers** — Mina reunites with Jedediah. Emotional scene; she stays permanently. Jedediah gives the party The Burnt Offering (10,000gp art object) as thanks. Corvus joins the party. The tetsubo repair requires infernal iron, which the party needs to source from the market.
4. **Shopping Spree** — Party is advised to shop by referral to avoid scams. Corvus, Jedediah, or Boss provides the first referral. Each merchant refers them onward. Party spends their 10,000gp across [[The Honored Blade]], [[The Earthen Vault]], [[The Spore Garden]], and [[The Eyrie]]. Keep an ear out for the warehouse hum throughout.
5. **Finding the Warehouse** — After shopping, party asks around. NPCs point them toward the Market Ward/Clerk's Ward border. They track the hum and locate [[Boss's Warehouse]].
6. **Inside the Warehouse** — Corvus leads the inspection (his obligation as security). Boss's condition for answering questions is that the inspection is completed. The warehouse takes a liking to the party. They clear the inspection, Boss answers their questions, and the warehouse becomes their base of operations.

### Critical Points

- Mina's departure is permanent. The scene at Bashers for Bashers is her exit.
- Corvus joins after Bashers for Bashers. He's with the party for the shopping and warehouse sections.
- The warehouse hum is background texture during shopping—it shouldn't feel like an urgent quest, just something they're passively aware of.
- Boss doesn't answer questions for free. Finding and inspecting the warehouse is the price, established early at Fork Off.
- The warehouse's sentience is subtle. It doesn't communicate. Its liking of the party manifests as the appearance of the party suite—something Boss notices and doesn't fully explain.
- A figure who looks exactly like Jig shadows the party throughout the session. He is never close enough to confront, never speaks, and disappears the moment anyone moves toward him or looks directly. Four sightings are marked throughout this document. He is purely atmospheric—do not explain him this session.

## Opening - The Sandstone District, Dawn

*The narrow street around the party is lined with clay and stone buildings, their facades weathered and damp. People move past without much interest. Some head to work, others finish night shifts. The air smells of dust, baked earth, and smoke. Water drips from a broken gutter. A few tents cluster in the shadows between buildings.*

*Close by, one building's door suddenly bursts outward. A figure crashes onto the cobblestones. A patron, dazed and bleeding. From inside the tavern comes the sound of breaking furniture, shouting, and the unmistakable crash of combat. Above the chaos, a single voice bellows out unhinged enthusiasm.* 

*"Crimson Slash!" Another crash of glass.* 

*"Midnight Reversal!" The sound of wood splintering.* 

*"Dragon's Ascent!" A roar of pain from someone.* 

*Then, louder and more violent than the rest: "GLORY HOLD!"* 

*The tavern's heavy oak door is scarred and warped. A faded sign hangs from a wrought-iron bracket above it, showing a brick colored red by flame. Through the open doorway, shadows move and collide. More furniture crashes. The smell of cheap ale, cooking oil, tobacco smoke, and now blood pours out onto the street.* 

*This... is the Burnt Brick.* 

*More folks fly out the front door of the tavern. Most appear to have a slightly demonic aspect: small horns, or reddish skin, forked tails, and almost all have a slight smell of brimstone to them. The street is gradually filling with more and more injured tavern patrons.*

> **SIGHTING 1** — As the party watches the chaos, anyone scanning the far side of the street notices a figure standing completely still among the moving crowd. He looks exactly like Jig—same build, same face, same bearing. He isn't watching the brawl. He's watching the party. The moment anyone looks directly at him or moves toward him, he steps backward into the shadow of a doorway and is gone. The doorway, if investigated, is empty.

*Then a figure emerges from the tavern, grappling with another patron. A man with wild reddish-orange hair swept up in a high ponytail, an eyepatch covering one eye. His face is alive with an unhinged grin. He drags the struggling patron out onto the street with one hand. His peg leg thumps against the cobblestones as he pivots. A massive tetsubo suddenly appears in his free hand, materialized from nowhere. He swings it in a smooth arc. The blow connects with a sickening thud. The patron crumples.*

*The man stands over the fallen figure, laughing. He doesn't notice the party at first, but as his gaze sweeps across the street, he locks onto them. His grin widens.*

*"More blood for the Brick!" he shouts, and gestures broadly at the chaos. "Come on then, new faces. This is the best part of morning in Sigil. Show me what you've got!"*

## What Happens Next

*Before anyone can move, a wooden chair comes flying out of the tavern doorway. It catches him across the back and shoulders. He crumples to the cobblestones. The chair clatters beside him. Dazed, groaning.*

*From inside the tavern, a gruff voice bellows: "That's enough of your bullshit, One-Eye! Get out and take your mess with you!"*

*Silence. The man lies in the street, stunned. The brawl continues inside, but out here, the challenge has ended before it began.*

- Party must decide what to do with the incapacitated man in the street
- When he comes to, he notices his metal tetsubo is badly dented from striking the helmeted patron's skull 
- Mina sees the broken weapon and recognizes an opportunity—she offers to repair it at her grandfather's smithy, [[Bashers for Bashers]] (establishing herself as a smith and securing her first customer)
- The man agrees but doesn't know where the smithy is
- **Party's Problem**: Mina doesn't know exactly where [[Bashers for Bashers]] is, only that it's somewhere in the Market Ward
- **Solution**: The man suggests asking his employer, Boss—he's an information broker and would know the smithy's location
- **Hook to Fork Off**: Party must visit Boss's shop to get information on [[Bashers for Bashers]]'s location
- This is exactly what the party needs—a reliable information broker in Sigil

## NPCs & Locations

### Mina's Grandfather: Jedediah Stonewood

*Jedediah Stonewood is a stout halfling in his late sixties, weathered and muscular from a lifetime of smithing. His iron-gray hair is worn long and pulled back. His hands are heavily scarred and calloused, one finger missing entirely. He speaks with a thick rural accent and moves with the unhurried confidence of a master craftsman.*

He's gruff and argumentative, especially with Mina, but it's clear he adores her. When they fight, it's spirited and passionate, neither willing to back down. He occasionally contracts with Boss to create precision tuning fork duplicates.

- **Smithy Location**: Somewhere in the Market Ward (exact location unknown to Mina)
- **Reward**: Upon hearing the party helped his granddaughter, Jedediah gives them a valuable art object worth approximately 10,000gp as payment
  - **The Burnt Offering**: An ornate decorative sculpture made of infernal steel, roughly 14 inches tall, shaped like stylized flames reaching upward in a graceful twist. Gemstones—rubies, garnets, and clear crystals—are embedded throughout to suggest embers, heat, and light. Purely decorative, meant for display. No magical properties or practical function. A wealthy collector or noble would readily pay 10,000gp for it.

### [[Fork Off]]

*A modest storefront in the Market Ward of Sigil. The sign is plain, with tiny lettering underneath indicating: "Tuning Forks & Planar Goods."*

*Inside, shelves display tuning forks in dozens of styles and materials, each labeled by plane. The shop also stocks a limited but carefully curated selection of magical artifacts useful for planar travel: protective amulets, scrolls of planeshift, gear designed for overcoming specific planar hazards.*

- **Location**: Market Ward
- **Proprietor**: [[Damroodleboss Delimimus]] (Boss)
- **Services**: Tuning fork sales, planar artifacts, information brokerage
- **Information Policy**: 
  - **Free**: Boss will freely tell the party where to find [[Bashers for Bashers]] (so Corvus can repair his tetsubo)
  - **Paid**: All other information requires payment—and Boss's price is unusual. He won't accept gold. Instead, he demands the party locate his missing warehouse and inspect it once found. The warehouse has been drifting unattended and he needs someone to check on his stock—nothing damaged, stolen, or gotten loose.
  - **The Deal**: Once the warehouse has been found and inspected, Boss will answer any questions the party has about Sigil, its locations, contacts, and services
- **Corvus's Connection**: Corvus works for Boss as a "minder" (warehouse security)
- **Corvus's Suggestion**: When Mina mentions needing to find her grandfather's smithy, Corvus suggests asking Boss—he's not just a shop owner, he's an information broker
- **Party's Interest**: This is exactly what the party has been seeking—a reliable information broker in Sigil

### Bashers for Bashers

*Jedediah Stonewood's smithy, located in the Market Ward. The name is a cant phrase—a forge where fighters get their weapons made and repaired. The workshop is built for work, not aesthetics: constant noise, heat, and sparks from the forge.*

- Located in the Market Ward (party must locate via Boss or investigation)
- A working forge and metalcraft shop
- Jedediah runs it with help from apprentices/hired hands
- Can repair or craft metal weapons and armor
- The tetsubo repair will take time (not immediate). It also requires infernal iron (not steel), which the party will need to locate in the market (Corvus joins the party). Mina stays behind, as this is effectively her permanent departure from the party.

> **SIGHTING 2** — At some point during the scene between Mina and Jedediah—ideally at its most emotional—a party member who glances toward the smithy's street-facing window sees the figure standing outside, looking in. Same face as Jig. Still. Watching. By the time anyone reacts, he has moved on. The street outside is empty in both directions.

### The Warehouse (Boss's Storage)

- Separate location from Fork Off storefront
- Houses Boss's inventory of tuning forks (limited stock due to space constraints)
- **Locating Mechanic**: The warehouse drifts between the Market Ward and Clerk's Ward -- it has a mind of its own, and can't really decide which ward is more appropriate for itself.
- **Finding It**: The warehouse emits a distinct hum audible from blocks away -- it does this until it is "found, " it's the warehouse's way of crying out for its owner.
  - NPCs in the area might mention hearing the hum
- **Current Status**: The warehouse is currently lost again (drifted unexpectedly) into the Market Ward
- **Party Assumption**: The party should keep an eye and ear out for the warehouse while going about other business in Sigil. They don't need to actively search for it immediately; they may stumble upon it or hear its distinctive hum while pursuing other objectives. This is a hook for later if the party needs to access Boss's full inventory or if time is running out on other matters.

## Merchants of the Market Ward

The party has approximately 10,000gp in treasure from Jedediah. Shopping for new equipment is a natural part of the session. The Market Ward contains several notable merchants.

**Referral System**: Before the party ventures into the Market Ward to shop, either [[Corvus]], [[Jedediah Stonewood|Jedediah]], or [[Damroodleboss Delimimus|Boss]] should advise them that shopping by referral is essential in Sigil. The Market Ward is rife with scammers, overpriced goods, and fraud. Getting proper referrals ensures honest dealing and preferential prices.

**How It Works**: 
- Depending on which items the party wants first (weapons, armor, potions, or magical items), they receive a referral to one of the four notable merchants below
- Each merchant is honest and well-regarded, guaranteeing fair trade
- Each merchant can provide referrals to the other merchants in turn, creating a chain of introductions
- This gives the party a sense of building connections in Sigil and understanding the city's social networks

> **SIGHTING 3** — At any point during the shopping spree, a party member catches a glimpse of a familiar face between market stalls—the figure, browsing a nearby vendor's wares with his back to the party. The moment they close the distance or call out, he rounds the corner of the stall. The vendor, if asked, doesn't remember serving him.

### [[The Honored Blade]]

*A weapons shop run by [[Kharravax]], a rakshasa. The shop is immaculate and displays masterwork weapons. Kharravax personally tests every piece before sale and treats commerce with the formality of a warrior's honor code.*

- **Specialty**: Masterwork weapons, rare blades, enchanted pieces
- **Notable**: Kharravax is intimidating but strictly professional. He respects skilled fighters and refuses to sell substandard goods regardless of profit.
- **Referrals**: Kharravax can refer the party to [[The Earthen Vault]] (armor), [[The Spore Garden]] (potions), or [[The Eyrie]] (magic items)

### [[The Earthen Vault]]

*An armor shop operated by Khrak, an earth elemental (xorn). The shop appears to phase partially through walls and floor. Gems and exotic metals are visible embedded in Khrak's hide and throughout the shop.*

- **Specialty**: Rare metals and ores, custom armor using planar materials
- **Notable**: Khrak prices by material rarity, not conventional value. It prefers barter to gold and will explain the planar origin of every material in meticulous detail.
- **Referrals**: Khrak can refer the party to [[The Honored Blade]] (weapons), [[The Spore Garden]] (potions), or [[The Eyrie]] (magic items)

### [[The Spore Garden]]

*A potions shop maintained by The Gardener, a myconid (fungal colony). The shop smells of earth and medicinal herbs. The Gardener speaks slowly, as if thinking through a network of connections.*

- **Specialty**: Potions, alchemical supplies, exotic reagents
- **Notable**: The Gardener is incapable of dishonesty and will only sell items it deems aligned with natural balance. All goods are verifiable and reliable.
- **Referrals**: The Gardener can refer the party to [[The Honored Blade]] (weapons), [[The Earthen Vault]] (armor), or [[The Eyrie]] (magic items)

### [[The Eyrie]]

*A magical curiosities shop run by Starwhisper, an ancient couatl. The shop appears more like a crystalline cave. The feathered serpent perches on massive stone coils, watching customers with eyes that seem to know too much.*

- **Specialty**: Wondrous items, planar artifacts, items with history
- **Notable**: Starwhisper judges customers by character, not coin. It will refuse sale to the corrupt, but may offer items below value to those it deems worthy.
- **Referrals**: Starwhisper can refer the party to [[The Honored Blade]] (weapons), [[The Earthen Vault]] (armor), or [[The Spore Garden]] (potions)

## Finding the Warehouse

After shopping, the party will likely start asking around for the warehouse. The following NPCs can provide information.

### Gromak (Dwarf spice merchant)
*A gruff, beard-braided dwarf who runs a spice stall and complains constantly about business.*

Heard the hum several times at night; thinks it's a planar disturbance. Last heard it coming from the direction of the warehouse district near the Market Ward's eastern edge.

### Lyra (Tiefling street urchin)
*A quick-witted fourteen-year-old tiefling who knows every alley and shortcut in the Market Ward.*

Has heard the hum many times and can sense when it's getting closer. Says it usually hides in the warehouse district or near the Clerk's Ward border, but moves around constantly.

### Torvin (Elderly scholar)
*An ink-stained human scholar wearing thick spectacles who spends his days studying planar phenomena.*

Suggests the warehouse is moving along planar resonance lines and recommends the party use a tuning fork to track it, as the warehouse resonates with the same frequencies.

### Marta (Half-orc tavern keeper)
*A matronly tavern owner who hears all the gossip from her patrons.*

Overheard adventurers mention the warehouse near the Clerk's Ward border about three days ago; can't say if it's still there or has drifted back toward the Market Ward.

### Kess (Gnome street musician)
*A cheerful gnome lute player with an unusual collection of trinkets.*

Has noticed the hum changes pitch depending on which ward it's in—higher and sharper near Clerk's Ward, lower and deeper near Market Ward—which could help the party track its current location.

### Varos (Tiefling city guard)
*A bored-looking guard stationed at the boundary between wards.*

Has observed the warehouse occasionally phasing through nearby buildings. Suggests checking the warehouse district or near the gate between the two wards where boundaries tend to be thinner.

It's the clearest look they've had all session—close enough to confirm every detail. He holds their gaze for a moment. Then he turns, walks around the corner of the building, and is gone. The alley behind the warehouse is a dead end. There is no one there.

## Entering the Warehouse

When the party locates the warehouse and reports back to Boss, he lays out two reasons they need to go inside before he'll answer any questions:

- **Corvus's obligation**: As hired security, Corvus is professionally obligated to inspect the warehouse after every unscheduled drift. He was going in regardless. The party comes with him.

The two motivations dovetail naturally—Corvus leads the inspection, Boss gets his peace of mind, and the party earns their answers.
