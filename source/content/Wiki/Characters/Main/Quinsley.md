## Backstory
Quinsley was born to a minor celestial lineage and once served in the court of Helm. When a child was prophesied to grow into a brutal tyrant, Quinsley refused to condemn someone for a future that hadn't happened yet, and stood up to protect them. Helm respected the choice, and Quinsley fell from the court by choice rather than punishment.

Since her fall, Quinsley spent most of her life as a sage before eventually taking work as a caravan guard, a job she held for eleven years. She never speaks of her past. On watch, she has a reputation for never sleeping — she simply stares into the dark, humming a lullaby from Arborea.

She eventually made her way to [[Underleaf]] after her time guarding caravans. She keeps up with current affairs, is committed to doing her part, and is fiercely protective of those she respects.

## (DM Only) Adventure Hooks
- **The Haunted Helm:** The owner of the Wandering Tits asks Quinsley to check a locked chest in the cellar, left by a dead adventurer who never returned. It radiates faint divine energy tied to Helm's domain of vigilance and protection. When Quinsley touches it, she recognizes it as a ceremonial helm from her old celestial order, left behind by a fellow Aasimar. It's cursed with a trapped memory — anyone who wears it sees flashes of the last owner's final stand against a shadowy cult still hunting for something the helm may be a key to. This is Quinsley's first tangible link to her old life in years, and she'll push hard to follow it.
- **The Stolen Watch:** Garret, the town's night watchman and Quinsley's regular drinking buddy, is framed for stealing a shipment of iron ingots. Quinsley won't let a good man rot — he's one of the only people who's ever treated her like a person rather than a curiosity — and asks the party to help track down the real thieves (a small-time smuggling ring) and clear his name.
- **The Debt of an Old Friend:** Grenda, a half-orc who served alongside Quinsley in a mercenary company before her caravan years, shows up at the Wandering Tits fleeing a rival company she broke contract with. She has a map to an abandoned temple of Helm containing celestial relics, and asks Quinsley to help her reach it and secure safety. Quinsley sees it as a chance to reclaim a piece of her old life and help an old friend who never judged her.
