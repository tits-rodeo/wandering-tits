[[Azhura]]'s head knight from her time holding her own march — military lieutenant, principal attendant, confidant, and advisor. Fought at her side, mounted as her rider, during the Siege of the Deep Gate, using gith-inspired tactics Azhura learned through [[Valraxis]]'s accounts. Her fate after Azhura's fall from grace is unknown.
