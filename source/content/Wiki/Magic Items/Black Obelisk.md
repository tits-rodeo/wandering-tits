**Magic Item**
_(Artifact)_

Black obelisks are monolithic artifacts scattered across Faerûn, each narrowing from a wide base to a pyramidal peak, ranging from fifteen to sixty feet tall depending on the instance. Their surfaces are described inconsistently from one obelisk to the next — some carved from obsidian, some from a polished rune-covered black stone, others from an unidentified black metal.

## Origin
The first obelisks were created by the [[Spellweaver|Spellweavers]] as a safeguard against catastrophic events threatening their empire. [[Vecna]] later stole one such obelisk, along with the knowledge of its creation, and used it to erase the Spellweavers from existence. That stolen knowledge eventually reached the arcanists of ancient Netheril, who built smaller, considerably weaker copies to power their flying enclaves. When Netheril fell, several of these copies were stolen or hidden away across the Realms.

## Powers
Every black obelisk radiates a faint abjuration aura, which any spellcaster can tap into to teleport themselves — and anyone else touching the obelisk — to a random location. Obelisks are known to react to *faerzress* in the Underdark and are wholly unaffected by Far Realm energies. At least two known obelisks have housed trapped extraplanar beings.

Full activation — the ability to alter time and reality on a grand scale, reversing the age of nearby creatures or sending an entire region centuries into its own past — requires a separate artifact of significant power to be "keyed into" the obelisk. Such an activation has never been confirmed to succeed.

## Known Obelisks
- **Nightstone**, the landmark around which the walled Sword Coast town was built.
- **Phandalin** — shattered long ago, its pieces scattered across the surrounding wilderness.
- **Shadowdusk Hold**, recovered from Undermountain by the disgraced Shadowdusk family.
- **Tomb of the Nine Gods**, in Chult, defaced by the lich Acererak.
- **Whorlstone Tunnels**, beneath Gracklstugh in the Underdark.
- **Ythryn**, buried beneath the Reghed Glacier — the only one on this list still fully intact, though damaged.

No source has ever confirmed a total count of obelisks in existence, nor the current location of the one Vecna stole and used against the Spellweavers.

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
None of the obelisks listed above are the true, original Spellweaver artifacts — every one is a lesser Netherese copy. A genuine, full-power original has never surfaced anywhere in the Realms.
