A holy relic of [[Bahamut]] said to possess the power to sever even curses laid by gods. Some who've traced the legend call it the Platinum Blade instead — the two names seem to refer to the same weapon.

## Origin
Rumored to have been left on Faerûn by Bahamut himself. Nothing about it is confirmed: whether "blade" is even literal, what it can truly cut beyond divine curses, who last wielded it, or why records of it are so scarce all remain open questions. 

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
[[Azhura]] believes it may be her only real chance at breaking her curse — assuming it exists at all.
