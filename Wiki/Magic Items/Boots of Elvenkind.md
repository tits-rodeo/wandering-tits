**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Uncommon)_ 
- While wearing these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you're moving across.
- You have advantage on Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) checks that rely on moving silently.

## (DM Only) Origin
Part of [[Wrinklefenderly Delimimus|Fen]]'s hoard — found rather than looted, by his usual divination-adjacent means. Given to the party during *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]* to help them move through occupied [[Underleaf]] without drawing a patrol's attention.
