**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Rare)_ 
- You can take a Magic action to choose a target within 30 feet. Psionic insight grants you a brief insight into the target's defenses. On your next turn, you gain advantage on your first attack roll against the target.
- _**Sentience.**_ The amulet is a sentient item with an Intelligence of 10, a Wisdom of 12, and a Charisma of 12. It has hearing and Darkvision out to 60 feet. Its alignment is Neutral Good. The amulet communicates by transmitting emotions, sending a tingling sensation through the wearer's mind when it wants to communicate something it has sensed. It can communicate through visions or dreams when the wearer is either in a trance or asleep.
- _**Personality.**_ The amulet has a personality similar to that of the princess who entrusted it. Once the amulet has decided on an owner, it believes that only that person should wear it, even if the bearer’s alignment differs from that of the amulet's or the bearer’s goals later clash with the amulet’s goals.
