Infiltration and/or assault on Ashra's occupied stronghold.

**(DM Only) Rose delivery routes.** Ashra stays through Vantha's execution, leaves for the Camp only after — no nightfall stealth window.
- **Plea:** a passed petition/token during a public mercy plea.
- **Prop:** tampered execution prop (rope, blade, ceremonial piece).
- **Tribute:** rides in with a town offering, delivered by an NPC proxy.
- **Crowd:** disguised PC gets a single contact moment mid-spectacle. Fallback.

---

# Hearth Hall - Common House
> *The public face of Ashra's occupation — what used to be the town's meeting hall, now a strongpoint. Furniture cleared out or broken down for barricades, a couple of cult soldiers posted at all times.*
- First room of the Hearth Hall climax, before Ashra herself is in sight.

## Encounter
- 1 thug

---

# Hearth Hall - Armory
> *Where Crimson Flame keeps its gear staged — weapon racks, a few crates of matériel from the camp outside. Picked over, not by design. 
> [[Vantha Emberlight|Vantha]], if still alive, is chained here — the room's the only one in Hearth Hall built to keep someone in.*
- If Vantha's alive: freeing her is the opening to plant, throw, or otherwise get the Moander rose onto or near Ashra directly. Single rose, no backup.
- If Vantha was executed on Day 2: the room's empty of her, cult soldiers using it as a break room instead.

---

# Hearth Hall - Meeting Chamber
> *The Circle's old chamber, repurposed — the long table still stands, but Ashra's claimed the head of it, as apparent by a tea cup familiar to Azhura.*
- Staging ground for any dialogue beat with Ashra before Phase 1 turns hostile.

## Encounter
- [[Ashra]]

# Hearth Hall - Office
> *Cramped, paper-choked, previously whichever Circle member handled the town's actual administration. Now rifled through — Ashra or her people have clearly been looking for something here.*
- Good place to drop a physical clue tying Ashra's search to Azhura.

## Encounter
- 1 fanatic
