A kobold butler who served [[Cinder]] during her captivity and entered [[Azhura]]'s service after Cinder's death, remaining loyal to her household until her imprisonment. He became the single most important agent in [[Valraxis]]'s covert operation to free her, using his position and remaining household contacts to arrange her escape before she could be fully stripped of everything she had.

He stayed within [[Crimson Flame]] territory for a time afterward, protected by Valraxis, but fled once the clan started hunting for a scapegoat to blame for Azhura's disappearance. He tracked her all the way to the temple where she later trained as a paladin. What became of him after that — whether he ever openly rejoined her, or what happened when that refuge burned — is unknown.
