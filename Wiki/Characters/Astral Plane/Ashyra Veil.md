## Backstory
Ashyra was once a priestess-diviner of [[Hecate]], goddess of magic, moons, and the dark of the night. Deep in her order's rites, she foresaw something even her own dread goddess did not want known — a fragment of a prophecy no church, no pantheon, wanted spoken aloud. Rather than reward her gift, her own faith turned on her, and word spread through divine channels she no longer understood. She fled not from one god's wrath, but from all of it — every deity's clergy alerted to hunt down a mortal who had seen too far.

She survived for a time by learning to steal what she could no longer be given: the ur-priest's trance, slipping into the space between a god and its worshiper to draw out power without ever being seen praying to anyone. It kept her alive, and kept her hidden — until she found her way, by accident or design, into the one place no god's attention could ever follow her: the belly of an astral dreadnought.

Once she'd built her own gate out of the donjon, she was no longer entirely trapped. Powerful and practiced enough to move quickly and unseen, she used it to slip back into Faerûn from time to time — never long enough to be noticed by the gods still hunting her, but long enough to keep researching in secret. On one such trip, she reached the ruins of a spell weaver colony allied with the gold dwarves of the Great Rift, and found something there that named a threat older and larger than anything she'd been hunted for: Atropus.

Her own account of much of this, in her own words, survives in [[The Journals and Papers of Ashyra Veil]], recovered from her study within the donjon.

## (DM Only) The Dark of It

Full backstory, the seed, the lead-lined portal room, and the donjon's Near Ethereal: see [[Banished Seed of Moander]].

- Built the gate out of the donjon herself, over ages of waiting for the dreadnought to consume the specific components she needed.
- Found the dormant seed of [[Moander]] already inside the donjon (banished there centuries earlier by [[Jergal]]'s agents) and cultivated it as a renewable divine battery, since the donjon cut her off from the wide, ambient theft her class normally relies on.
- Remained uninfected for most of that time — until her own long isolation and negligence let the seed slip past her control and begin infecting her directly, centuries later than it should have.
- Killed herself rather than let the infection complete and rise as a true avatar wearing her body. It stopped the seed from becoming whole, but not the half-finished transformation her death interrupted — with no host left to complete in, it spread outward into the donjon itself instead, seeding the entire demiplane with an unfinished, uncontrolled corruption.
- Now a ghost, anchored by unfinished business (equal parts the prophecy she can't stop repeating and the escape she never completed in life).
- Her original forbidden vision (whatever got her hunted by every church in the first place) and her Atropus knowledge are two separate things — the Atropus warning was learned later, on a research trip to a spell weaver colony allied with the Great Rift's gold dwarves, made possible only because she'd already built her own gate out of the donjon.
- The prophecy she forces out of possessed hosts — including the "coming of Atropus" warning given to [[Trixie]] — draws on that later-acquired knowledge, not the original vision that got her exiled.
- Name, former faith ([[Hecate]]), and era are set; anything else about her mortal life (order, home, the exact original forbidden vision, and exactly what she found at the spell weaver ruins) is still open to decide.
- Her holy symbol of Hecate (a setting moon, bent and broken but still whole) is what doubles as the donjon gate's key/focus -- see [[Banished Seed of Moander]] and [[Kabuki Tok Tok|Tok]]'s portal key.
