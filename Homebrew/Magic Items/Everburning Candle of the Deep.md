**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Common)_ 
- This item appears to be identical to a regular black candle on a silver stand. However, when the wick is pinched, it lights up with a blue flame and continues burning until it is snuffed out in the same motion.
- The candle provides bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet.
- The candle is not put out if submerged in water, and the candle wax never reduces in size.
