[[Skaerth]]'s former mate and mother of [[Ashra]] and [[Infernia]]. Grew jealous of Skaerth's attention toward the captive [[Cinder]], tormented her, and fought with Skaerth repeatedly over it before finally leaving the [[Sword Mountains]] after one last confrontation. Ashra and Infernia sided with their father rather than follow her. Nothing has been heard from her since — even within the clan, her current whereabouts and fate are a complete mystery.
