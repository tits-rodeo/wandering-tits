**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Uncommon)_ 
_Requires Attunement_
- These gloves protect you from ranged weapon attacks.
- **Activation:** Reaction, when you would be hit by a ranged weapon attack. Reduce the damage from that attack by 1d10 + your Dexterity modifier. If this reduces the damage to 0, and the attack used a projectile small enough for you to hold in one hand, you catch it.

## (DM Only) Origin
Part of [[Wrinklefenderly Delimimus|Fen]]'s hoard — found rather than looted, by his usual divination-adjacent means. Given to the party during *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]*.
