The brain of the Meat Train sloughed off a portion of its brain at some point, due to it wanting to shed the part of itself that was subservient and fearful. It is friendly toward [[Conrad]] due to his affinity for magic. Is terrified of [[Francis]], having memories of his "mind hacking" of the Meat Train. 

After being separated, the small portion of brain melded with and took over a snake petitioner (from whom it took its surname) for the sake of locomotion, its personality melding with it to become somewhat more altruistic.

It retains some of the more recent memories of the original brain, but only its surface motivations.

## Special Properties
Can impose a fear effect on creatures of weak mind (assuming they aren't already under some other mental effects). May have additional special properties.
