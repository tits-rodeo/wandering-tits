### Laboratory
_Level 9 Bastion Facility_

**Prerequisite:** None
**Space:** 16 squares
**Hirelings:** 1
**Order:** Craft

A Laboratory contains storage space for alchemical supplies and workspaces for crafting various concoctions.

**_Craft Options._** When you issue the Craft order to this facility, choose one of the following options:

- **Craft: Alchemist’s Supplies.** The facility’s hireling crafts anything that can be made with Alchemist’s Supplies using the rules in the Player’s Handbook and Chapter 7 of the Dungeon Master's Guide.
- **Craft: Poison.** You commission the facility’s hireling to craft a vial containing one application of a poison. The poison must be one of the following: Burnt Othur Fumes, Essence of Ether, or Torpor. This work takes 7 days, and you must pay half the poison’s cost. See Poison in Chapter 3 of the Dungeon Master's Guide for descriptions and costs of poisons.
