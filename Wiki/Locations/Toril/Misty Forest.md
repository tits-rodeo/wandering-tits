The Misty Forest lies west of the High Moor and south of the river Delimbiyr, a fog-bound, rain-soaked woodland that also serves as an elven kingdom in its own right.

## Description
Perpetual mist and steady rain cling to the Misty Forest, giving the place its name. Pine and evergreen cover most of the woodland, with spruce dominating the northern stretches. Red deer are the most common game, rarely straying from the tree line except in summer, while bears, boars, and wild cattle roam the underbrush. Owlbears are known to lair here as well.

## Society
Scattered shrines to Eilistraee, Eldath, Mielikki, and Silvanus dot the forest, remnants of sacred sites that date back to the ancient elven realm of Miyeritar. A druid-tended temple to Chauntea also stands within the trees. Most notable is a hidden pool sacred to Eldath, believed to have once opened onto her divine realm of the True Grove before the Time of Troubles severed the connection -- her faithful still hold that the pool's gate could be reawakened.

The forest's elves keep bees and trade heavily in mead and honey wine, meeting outside merchants at the Way Inn to barter for goods they cannot produce themselves.

## Government
By the late 15th century DR, the Misty Forest is ruled by King Melandrach, the self-styled "King of the Woods," who has held the throne for over 150 years and insists the realm belongs to elves alone. Rangers patrol its borders, and druids tend groves scattered throughout the wood, while a small community of wild elves and wood elves -- along with the elusive, deer-like hybsils -- make their homes among the trees. Barbarians and orcs from the neighboring High Moor also make use of the forest, the former for food and timber, the latter as a base to raid caravans on the nearby Trade Way.

## History
The Misty Forest was once part of Miyeritar, one of the elven realms shattered during the Crown Wars. Its people were later drawn into the Second Dragonspear War, but by 1368 DR had rebuilt everything they'd lost. In 1363 DR, following a brutal conflict with baatezu, the High Forest elves sent the advisor Eamond Blackmantle to serve as the Misty Forest's Speaker; his efforts to organize reconstruction and open trade turned the shattered realm into the largest settlement of wild elves west of the Inner Sea.

In 1372 DR, a tribe of Chultan wild elves led by Kyleena Moonglow settled in the forest after a failed attempt to seize the Lonely Barrens.

During the Tyranny of Dragons, King Melandrach represented both the Misty Forest and the High Forest at the Council of Waterdeep -- where it came to light that his son Neronvain, long believed dead, was alive and had become the Cult of the Dragon's green Wyrmspeaker, terrorizing his father's own people.
