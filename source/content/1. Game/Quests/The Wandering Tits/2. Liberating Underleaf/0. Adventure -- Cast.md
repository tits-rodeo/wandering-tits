## Cast

### [[Oswin Marrow]] (the Grocer)
> *Tiefling, sharp-eyed, ledger always in hand, apron dusted with flour*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Gus Fring (*Breaking Bad*) — composed exterior, grief underneath, cracks not outbursts.
- **Voice:** clipped, transactional — cracks when Garrek comes up.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** Garrek's old ledger still open on the counter.
- **Home:** runs the general store; Garrek's room in back — took him in as apprentice, and a son.
- **Purpose:** tunnel gatekeeper. Withholds until the party genuinely reckons with Garrek's death, not a hollow apology.

### [[Brynn Thatcher]] (the Brewer)
> *Water genasi, damp-skinned, sleeves always rolled up, smells faintly of hops*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Joyce Byers (*Stranger Things*) — warm, protective, convinced she's out of her depth.
- **Voice:** direct, rueful, trails off admitting she doesn't know what she's doing.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** `"None of us actually know what we're doing"` — an invitation, not a complaint.
- **Home:** runs the Brewery; basement is resistance/party HQ.
- **Purpose:** host, not leader — resistance has no one in charge until the party steps up.

### [[Fen|Wrinklefenderly "Fen" Delimimus]] (the Local Wizard)
> *Gnome, ancient, cluttered robes hung with half-identified trinkets, permanently squinting at something only he can see*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Doc Brown energy — eccentric hoarder-inventor, rambling, brilliant underneath. One of [[Damroodleboss Delimimus|Doc Delimimus]]'s children; his school is Abjuration.
- **Voice:** distracted, mutters, answers the question he wishes you'd asked.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** hoard found via divination, half never identified.
- **Home:** cluttered cottage at the forest's edge; quieter path from Renn's Smithy.
- **Purpose:** supplies an early magic item or two. (Not meant to die here — see bastion plan.)

### [[Skritt Emberquill]] (the Local Artist)
> *Kobold, ink-stained claws, tail perpetually flicking paint, a portrait pinned up for nearly every household in town*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Adrian Monk energy — obsessive, notices what's wrong before anything else.
- **Voice:** rapid, detail-obsessed, more animated the more observational it gets.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** describes a face by its asymmetries before its expression.
- **Home:** a studio in town, walls covered in portraits and sketches.
- **Purpose:** spot-the-difference puzzle — his sketches expose the Watcher (Coronatid agent hunting Mantis, distinct from the Hunter at the Wooded Pathway).

### [[Mireille Duskweave]] (the Fortune Teller)
> *Harengon, long ears in constant motion, wrapped in mismatched shawls, lives out of a parked carriage near the Cemetery*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Luna Lovegood energy — dreamy, says unsettling true things. Harengon prey-alertness reads as spaciness.
- **Voice:** soft, unhurried, non sequiturs that turn out not to be.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** carriage sits by the Cemetery; hasn't been wrong yet.
- **Home:** the Carriage — parked, arrival unremembered by anyone.
- **Purpose:** trust nets a reading with real finale intel. If the party's still carrying the rose, she also IDs it and drops Moander lore.

### [[Perce Hollin]] (Circle Member, deceased)
> *Human, weathered from years on the Trade Way, a caravan master's coat that never quite lost the road dust*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** the practical logistics guy — caravans, fair deals, no flair.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** already dead on arrival — Ashra's first example; pyre visible from the Burned Clearing.
- **Home:** handled Trade Way logistics and caravan deals.
- **Purpose:** opens the adventure on a death, not a threat.

### [[Vantha Emberlight]] (Circle Member, at risk)
> *Dragonborn, scarred, carries herself like she's still expecting an inspection*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** the Circle's defense voice — ex-soldier instincts nobody listened to.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** argued pre-occupation for real defenses. Ignored.
- **Home:** no trade; Circle seat on military credibility.
- **Purpose:** at risk Day 2 sundown — her death (if it happens) proves the clock is real.

### [[Iris Bellwether]] (the Resistance Runner)
> *Goblin, quick, patched leathers, knows every back path in and out of town*
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Morty energy — nervy, in over her head, gets it done anyway.
- **Voice:** urgent, breathless, rushed.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** intercepts the party before they charge into town.
- **Home:** attached to the Brewery resistance cell.
- **Purpose:** opening contact — redirects the party to Renn's Smithy.
