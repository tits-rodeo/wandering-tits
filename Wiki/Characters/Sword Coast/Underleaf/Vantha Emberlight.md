## Background
A scarred dragonborn ex-soldier who sits on Underleaf's Circle on the strength of her old military experience — the closest thing the Circle had to a defense-minded voice. She argued, before the [[Crimson Flame]] occupation, that Underleaf needed some kind of real defense. Nobody acted on it.

Under [[Ashra]]'s ultimatum — hand over the dragon-blooded woman who worked at [[The Wandering Tit]], or Circle members die until she's found — Vantha is next in line after [[Perce Hollin]], at risk of execution on the second day of *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]* if the party hasn't meaningfully disrupted the occupation by then.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** carries herself like she's still expecting an inspection — old soldier instincts nobody quite listened to until it was too late.
- **Purpose:** Her death, if it happens, is the clearest sign to the party that the execution clock is real and not a bluff.
