### Gaming Hall
_Level 9 Bastion Facility_

**Prerequisite:** None
**Space:** 36 squares
**Hirelings:** 4
**Order:** Trade

A Gaming Hall offers recreational activities like chess and games of darts, cards, or dice.

**_Trade: Gambling Hall._** When you issue the Trade order to this facility, the facility’s hirelings turn the Gaming Hall into a gambling den for 7 days. At the end of the seventh day, roll 1d100 and consult the following table to determine your portion of the house’s winnings.

|1d100|Winnings|
|---|---|
|01–50|1d6 × 10 GP|
|51–85|2d6 × 10 GP|
|86–95|4d6 × 10 GP|
|96–00|10d6 × 10 GP|
