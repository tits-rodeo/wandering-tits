Elder brother of [[Azhura]] and heir, somewhat sympathetic to Azhura but ultimately loyal to the [[Crimson Flame]], he may become an unexpected ally at times. Son of [[Skaerth]] and [[Vaelithrax]], who rejected Skaerth's hierarchical ambitions and left him under his father's control. Hatched around 1000 DR.

Around 1200 DR he left to serve roughly two centuries with gith dragon legions, returning to the Crimson Flame around 1400 DR — his absence deepened [[Cinder]]'s isolation while he was away, and his return put him back in place not long before Azhura's birth in 1410 DR.

After [[Cinder]] (Skaerth's last mate and Azhura's mother) died while Azhura was still a whelp, Valraxis was the one who actually raised her alongside the clan's kobolds, since Skaerth had no interest in doing so himself. He shielded her from the worst of the clan's cruelty when he could; on raids, she was only used as a meat shield by her sisters when he wasn't present.

During the Siege of the Deep Gate, he arrived late (deliberately delayed by Skaerth) to kill the invading black dragon commander and capture its surviving juveniles, after Azhura had already nearly killed herself dueling it with the incomplete Starfire Lance. He later arranged for [[Lord Halvern]], who had personally administered Azhura's "correction and training" after the Feast Below, to be reassigned to a dangerous northern garrison.

He objected when Skaerth cursed and imprisoned Azhura but was overruled. Rather than accept her permanent imprisonment, he secretly arranged her escape using his own hidden agents together with [[Harka]] and what remained of her household, funding the operation with part of her own hoard and hiding the rest in a cache reachable only through a secret passage inside his own lair. That treason against his father remains undiscovered.

As heir, he commands major military forces and may act as regent when Skaerth delegates authority — he'd be the recognized successor if Skaerth were ever killed or incapacitated. He genuinely believes the Crimson Flame can be reformed from within and still hopes to one day bring Azhura back into a safer version of the clan, though he recognizes the arrangement is unstable and that he may eventually have to choose between her and the clan.

He still visits her in secret from time to time. She's noticed, but lets him believe she hasn't.
