*The following text is translated from Thri-Kreen*

I have decided to try my hand at this journaling process Azhura and Quinsley seem so fond of. I will be writing in my home language, as I find the so-called Common-speak to be quite lacking in the romance of Thri-Kreen. I have tried keeping my telepathic abilities secret for a while, but I have opened up to my companions, even if it was not deliberate. Writing in Thri-Kreen will also prevent my companions from reading about my deepest thoughts. 

As much as I have been fond of Mr. [[Tok Tok|Tik Tok]] and [[Azhura]] and the others in the past, their recent actions have made me question how much I can trust them. Why did Mr. Tik Tok bring us into that alternate realm? Surely there were better ways to escape the fire. 

Speaking of the fire, Azhura and her connection to these cultists is suspicious for sure, but I am sympathetic to wanting to run away from the past. If my people ever found me, would my companions be as forgiving? 

It has been a while since I ruminated on the past this much. It does not feel good. As much as it would be easier to run away from these feelings, I may need to confront them at some point. 

I do miss the Princess. She was always someone I could pour my heart out to. I wonder if she would forgive me for running away… 

Anyway, our new companion [[Quinsley]] is capable at best, but I do not know enough about her to trust her either. 

At this point, the only one I trust is [[Trixie]], but that may be because she does not seem intelligent enough to plot against me in any way. She slept through the entire escape from the realm. I quite honestly don’t know what to think of this. 

I have not been able to satiate my bloodlust despite the handful of opponents that have fell to my arsenal of weapons. I hunger for more. These weapons are strong and I use them well, yet I miss my collection from home. Perhaps I will have to start a new collection. 

We have recently acquired a [[Bag of Holding]] in which I could keep my weapons and trophies I collect in the future. I need more! 

This was fun. I enjoy this journaling process. Perhaps I shall do more.

#### - [[Mantis]]
