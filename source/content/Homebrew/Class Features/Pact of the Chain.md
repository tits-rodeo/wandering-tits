## Variant: Familiar Attacks as Player's Bonus Action
Those with the **Pact of the Chain** feature normally have to sacrifice one of their own attacks in order to allow their familiar to deliver an attack.

With this variant rule, the owner of the familiar can instead choose to sacrifice their Bonus Action in order to allow their familiar to attack.

The owner of the familiar can choose to sacrifice either their Bonus Action or a single attack, but both options cannot be taken in the same round (the familiar is still limited to a single attack per round).
