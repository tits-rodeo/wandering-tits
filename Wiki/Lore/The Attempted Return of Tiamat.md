A chronological account of the [[Cult of the Dragon]]'s campaign to raise a ransom hoard vast enough to draw [[Tiamat]] out of her prison in the Nine Hells and back onto Toril, and of the war that followed to stop her once that hoard was spent. This entry covers the full arc: the hoard-gathering campaign from Greenest to Skyreach Castle (*Hoard of the Dragon Queen*), and the subsequent Council of Waterdeep's war against the Cult of the Dragon through to Tiamat's banishment at the Well of Dragons (*Rise of the Dragon Queen*).

## Background
Under **Severin Silrajin**, a reformist faction of the Cult of the Dragon abandoned the order's old fixation on dracolich apotheosis in favor of a new mandate straight from Tiamat herself: assemble a hoard of stolen wealth large enough to serve as tribute, use it to complete the *Mask of the Dragon Queen*, and use the Mask to drag Tiamat's temple out of Avernus and onto the Material Plane. The Cult allied openly with chromatic dragons and with the **Red Wizards of Thay**, and organized itself under regional **Wyrmspeakers** — chief among them **Galvan the Blue** and **Severin the Red** — each overseeing raids across separate stretches of the Sword Coast.

## Greenest in Flames
The campaign opened with a raid on the border town of **Greenest**, hit in a single night by cultist raiders and the adult blue dragon **Lennithon**. A band of adventurers passing through — drawn in by Governor **Nighthill**'s call for aid — helped evacuate townsfolk to the keep, held the gates, and were forced to talk their way out of a confrontation with Lennithon rather than fight him outright. Before withdrawing, the Cult's champion **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath**, a half-dragon warrior, issued a formal challenge and dueled one of the adventurers. The raid's true purpose only became clear afterward: it was a smash-and-grab, not a conquest — the Cult stripped the town of coin and valuables and moved on before dawn.

## The Raiders' Camp and the Dragon Hatchery
Following the retreating raiders, the adventurers located a Cult staging camp too large to assault outright and infiltrated it to recover **Leosin Erlanthar**, a monk being held captive there. Erlanthar had already pieced together the shape of the Cult's plan and became the party's main source of insight into it going forward. A subsequent push into a hatchery cave beneath the camp confirmed that Greenest's stolen wealth was already gone, shipped out ahead of them, and turned up a chamber of dragon eggs guarded by a roper — as well as a rematch with Cyanwrath. With the camp abandoned, the adventurers set out to follow the money north.

## On the Road
What followed was a two-month pursuit of a single hidden treasure wagon, hiding itself inside ordinary merchant caravans, from Greenest up through **Elturel** and **Baldur's Gate**. In Elturel, the paladin **Ontharr Frume** — a member of the **Order of the Gauntlet** — gave the party a contact and passage north. In Baldur's Gate, they traced the wagon to a Cult safehouse run by the spy **Aravax Foxtraveler**, and confirmed the hoard's next destination: **Waterdeep**. From there the chase continued overland past the Fields of the Dead, Troll Claws, and Dragonspear Castle toward the City of Splendors.

## Waterdeep and the Council Quiet
In Waterdeep, Leosin Erlanthar formalized the resistance to the Cult by founding the **Council Quiet**, a coalition of concerned factions and individuals working to track and disrupt the hoard's movement; the adventurers were invited to join as voting members. The gnome agent **Jemna Gleamsilver** supplied the Council's clearest picture yet of the Cult's leadership, naming Wyrmspeakers Galvan the Blue and Severin the Red as the operation's regional commanders. From Waterdeep, the party followed the hoard's trail roughly ten days north along the High Road to the **Carnath Roadhouse**, where they learned the hoard was being funneled into the **Mere of Dead Men**.

## Castle Naerytar
In the Mere, the party tracked lizardfolk servants of the Cult to **Castle Naerytar**, a swamp fortress caught in a three-way dispute: the black dragon **Voaraghamanthar** wanted the swamp back under his own control; the native lizardfolk resented the bullywugs the Cult had put over them; and the Cult's agent **Borngray** ran the castle's operations from its central tower. The adventurers infiltrated the castle, recovered the black-dragon-skinned tome *Dark Sermons of Strongor Bonebinder* and a Ring of Myrkul, fought through the bullywug leader **Pharblex Spattergoo**, and took Borngray's portal-key amulet to access a teleportation portal hidden in the caves beneath the castle.

## The Hunting Lodge
The portal delivered the party to a remote hunting lodge — outwardly an elven resort belonging to the **Erondale family**, in truth a Cult way station used by the agent **Talis** to stockpile hoard money before its final leg to Skyreach. Unbeknownst to Talis, the Cult agent **Varrim** had already turned her own guards against her, intending to have her killed once the last of the hoard passed through. The lodge served chiefly as a stopover before the party pressed on toward the village of **Parnast**.

## Skyreach Castle
Parnast itself had fallen under Cult control by the time the party arrived. Beyond it waited **Skyreach Castle**, a fortress kept aloft by the cloud giant **Blagothkus** and bound to the will of his dead wife **Esclarotta**'s spirit. Inside, the adventurers confronted the Cult's assembled leadership at the height of its power: Blagothkus himself, the Red Wizard **Rath Modar**, the dragon-mask-bearing commander **Rezmir**, and an adult white dragon guarding the vaults. Seizing or destroying the hoard here dealt the Cult's plan a severe setback — Severin's operation had spent months and untold lives assembling exactly this treasure to buy Tiamat's return, and losing it here bought the Realms time before the Cult could try again.

## The Council of Waterdeep
Losing the hoard at Skyreach cost the Cult badly, but didn't end it. With Leosin Erlanthar's Council Quiet folded into a larger effort, the surviving factions of the Sword Coast, the Harpers, the Order of the Gauntlet, the Emerald Enclave, the Lords' Alliance, and others, convened the **Council of Waterdeep** to coordinate a war against the Cult directly. The adventurers were deputized with emergency investigative powers and sent out on a string of missions between council sessions: tracking down the Cult's remaining Wyrmspeakers, courting the metallic dragons as allies, and hunting the five Dragon Masks the Cult needed to complete the *Mask of the Dragon Queen*. Word soon reached the council of the Cult's ultimate goal: sounding the **Draakhorn**, an ancient relic capable of rallying chromatic dragons to Tiamat's cause across all of Faerûn.

## The Sea of Moving Ice
The Draakhorn's last known location put the party on the trail of **Maccath the Crimson**, a tiefling sorcerer of the Arcane Brotherhood who vanished three years prior while investigating it, last seen near an iceberg lair belonging to the white dragon **Arauthator** ("Old White Death"), a staunch Cult ally. The adventurers tracked her to Arauthator's lair at **Oyaviggaton**, where she'd been held prisoner and forced to decipher magic Arauthator had looted from the Hosttower of the Arcane generations earlier. Arauthator had intended to hand her over to his grieving mate **Arveiaturace** as a replacement for her dead wizard-companion. The party drove Arauthator off or killed him, freed Maccath, and recovered the stolen Hosttower lore, earning the Arcane Brotherhood's allegiance in the process.

## Death to the Wyrmspeakers: Varram
Word reached the Harpers that the White Wyrmspeaker, **Varram the White**, had gone missing from Cult lines: his White Dragon Mask had been stolen by a Zhentarim-allied thief, and he'd staged a cover story to search for it in secret before Severin noticed it was gone. The trail led the party from Boareskyr Bridge into the Serpent Hills, to the **Tomb of Diderius**, a wizard's crypt built around an ancient divination pool, now infested by a yuan-ti colony called Ss'tck'al. The party found Varram already a captive of the yuan-ti, tortured for the wealth he'd promised them, and either rescued him or let him die. Taken alive, Varram broke, offering the council insight into the Cult's hierarchy and confirming that the White Dragon Mask had already been recovered and moved to the Well of Dragons.

## Death to the Wyrmspeakers: Neronvain
See *[[Neronvain]]* for the fuller account. In brief: the Green Wyrmspeaker's raids against his own kin in the Misty Forest, in pursuit of tribute for the Mask of the Dragon Queen, led the party to his stronghold and a confrontation with him and his dragon ally Chuth. Neronvain was taken alive and handed over to the council and to his father, King Melandrach; Chuth broke off and fled to report the loss to Severin.

## The Cult Strikes Back
Once the Cult recognized the adventurers as a genuine threat, it moved to eliminate them directly, in three escalating attempts. The first was a probing strike meant as a warning, and failed outright. The second, months later, sent a black dragon and summoned yugoloth mercenaries (hired through the Cult's Red Wizard allies) against the party in a public place, an assault meant to kill rather than intimidate. The third and final attempt came only after the party had proven twice unkillable: a full flight of young red dragons, backed by cultists and Red Wizards of Rath Modar's splinter sect, thrown at the party wherever they made their home. All three failed.

## Metallic Dragons, Arise
The silver dragon **Otaaryliakkarnos**, operating in Waterdeep under the guise of a human woman named Elia, invited the council to send an envoy to a council of five ancient metallic dragons in the Nether Mountains: Otaaryliakkarnos herself, the gold dragon **Protanther**, the bronze dragon **Nymmurh**, and the copper dragon **Tazmikella**, among them. The party was chosen to represent the Sword Coast's factions. Through negotiation, favors, and concessions on the council's behalf, the adventurers secured the metallic dragons' support against the Cult and Tiamat's chromatic allies at the Well of Dragons.

## Xonthal's Tower
A message reached the party from **Iskander**, second-in-command of a dracolich-traditionalist faction holed up in the long-sealed **Xonthal's Tower**, claiming paranoid fear for his life and offering to defect, delivering the Blue Dragon Mask in exchange for rescue. The party infiltrated the tower's hedge maze and dungeons, only to find Iskander already dead, killed by the tower's own guardian elementals before they arrived, and the "Blue Dragon Mask" a deliberate forgery Severin had planted to placate the traditionalists led by **Jorgen Pawl**. Escaping the tower, the party was forced to fight off **Lennithon**, the adult blue dragon from Greenest, sent by Jorgen Pawl in reprisal. The forged mask's discovery back in Waterdeep raised uncomfortable questions about a possible Cult spy inside the council itself, questions that were never fully answered.

## Mission to Thay
With the Cult's plan resting heavily on its Red Wizard allies, exiles from Thay under **Rath Modar**, the council accepted an invitation from the lich-lord **Szass Tam**'s regime to send envoys directly into Thay. The party met with **Tharchion Eseldra Yeth** at Nethwatch Keep, endured a night of magically enforced interrogation at the hands of Thayan Red Wizards, and secured Thay's aid against Rath Modar's splinter sect, delivered afterward through the Red Wizard liaison **Nyh Ilmichh**.

## Tiamat's Return
With Skyreach Castle's hoard denied to the Cult and its Wyrmspeakers dead, captured, or exposed, Severin's plan reached its final stage regardless: a full-scale assault by the massed forces of the Sword Coast against the Cult's army at the **Well of Dragons**, where the Cult had raised Tiamat's temple from the earth itself. While armies of the Lords' Alliance, the Order of the Gauntlet, the Emerald Enclave, and their allied giants, devils, and metallic dragons clashed with the Cult's chromatic dragons and mercenaries outside, the adventurers fought their way into the temple sanctuary itself, where Severin, wearing the completed Mask of the Dragon Queen, oversaw the summoning ritual alongside Rath Modar and his Red Wizards.

The party disrupted enough of the ritual, the mask, the sacrifices, the ritual's focus, that Tiamat emerged into the world critically weakened. She did not reward her servants: the instant she manifested in full, she devoured Rath Modar, his surviving Red Wizards, and Severin himself (mask and all) before turning on the adventurers. Reduced by the party's efforts beforehand, the Dragon Queen was beaten back through the ritual's collapsing portal and banished to Avernus, where she remains bound until someone recreates the conditions to try to free her again.

## Key Figures
- **Severin Silrajin** — Wyrmspeaker and leader of the reformist Cult of the Dragon; the campaign's chief architect. Devoured by Tiamat the moment she manifested, mask and all.
- **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** — Half-dragon champion of the Cult, met at Greenest and again in the dragon hatchery.
- **Leosin Erlanthar** — Monk and information broker; founder of the Council Quiet, later folded into the Council of Waterdeep.
- **Rezmir** — Cult commander entrusted with the Black Dragon Mask; present at Skyreach.
- **Rath Modar** — Red Wizard of Thay allied with the Cult; led the summoning ritual at the Well of Dragons and was devoured alongside Severin.
- **Blagothkus** — Cloud giant lord of Skyreach Castle, bound to serve the Cult by the ghost of his wife Esclarotta.
- **Ontharr Frume** — Paladin of the Order of the Gauntlet; early ally of the party in Elturel.
- **Galvan the Blue** — Wyrmspeaker overseeing regional raids.
- **[[Neronvain]]** — Green Wyrmspeaker, exiled son of King Melandrach of the Misty Forest; captured and handed to the council.
- **Chuth** — Neronvain's green dragon ally; escaped the stronghold, whereabouts unresolved.
- **Varram the White** — White Wyrmspeaker, broken and turned informant after his capture by the party.
- **Maccath the Crimson** — Tiefling sorcerer of the Arcane Brotherhood, rescued from Arauthator's lair.
- **Otaaryliakkarnos** — Silver dragon ambassador who brokered the metallic dragons' support.
- **Iskander** — Would-be defector at Xonthal's Tower, dead before the party arrived, a Cult casualty rather than a Cult loss.
- **Jorgen Pawl** — Leader of the Cult's dracolich-traditionalist faction; sent Lennithon after the party in reprisal.
- **Eseldra Yeth** and **Nyh Ilmichh** — Thayan tharchion and Red Wizard liaison who secured Thay's support against Rath Modar's splinter sect.

## Organizations Involved
- **[[Cult of the Dragon]]** — orchestrated the entire campaign, from the hoard-gathering at Tiamat's instruction through her summoning at the Well of Dragons.
- **[[Church of Tiamat]]** — maintains agents inside the Cult, though largely working at cross-purposes to Severin's faction during this campaign.
- **Red Wizards of Thay** — Rath Modar's exile faction allied with the Cult; a rival, council-aligned Thayan faction under Szass Tam later helped bring them down.
- **Order of the Gauntlet** — opposed the Cult throughout; supplied allies like Ontharr Frume and frontline troops at the Well of Dragons.
- **Council Quiet** — resistance coalition founded in Waterdeep by Leosin Erlanthar; absorbed into the Council of Waterdeep once the wider war effort began.
- **Council of Waterdeep** — the coalition of Sword Coast factions (Lords' Alliance, Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, Emerald Enclave, and others) that directed the war against the Cult.
- **Arcane Brotherhood** — allied with the council after the party rescued Maccath the Crimson and returned lore stolen from the Hosttower of the Arcane.
- **Metallic dragon council** — five ancient metallic dragons who pledged their kind's aid against Tiamat's chromatic allies.
- **Zhentarim** — nominally neutral throughout, but supplied assassins to the final battle and brokered deals around Xonthal's Tower.

## Open Threads
- Neronvain's fate after being handed to the council and to King Melandrach was left unresolved; so was Chuth's, last known to still be at large. See *[[Neronvain]]*.
- The forged Blue Dragon Mask at Xonthal's Tower raised the possibility of a Cult spy inside the Council of Waterdeep itself. Never confirmed or ruled out.
- Skyreach Castle remains in the party's hands, a potential base or complication for whatever follows.
- Tiamat is banished, not destroyed. She remains bound in Avernus until someone recreates the ritual's conditions.
- Hundreds of unrepentant Cult loyalists, mercenaries, and opportunists scattered from the Well of Dragons after Tiamat's banishment, along with a fortune in unclaimed treasure still sitting in the ruins.
