# The Honored Blade

A weapons shop in the Market Ward run by an extraordinary proprietor.

## Proprietor: Kharravax (Rakshasa)

*A massive tiger-like demon standing upright on hind legs, approximately 9 feet tall. His fur is deep amber with black stripes. He wears fine silks and rings of power on every finger. His claws are filed to points and his teeth are filed to needle-sharpness. His eyes are intelligent and calculating.*

A rakshasa nobleman operating independently from his infernal masters. He trades in weapons with the understanding of someone who's killed thousands. Views combat as an art form and respects only weapons (and wielders) of exceptional quality. Demands high prices but backs them up—he personally tests every weapon before selling. Speaks with courtly formality masking ruthless pragmatism. 

His honor code is alien but absolute: he never sells a weapon he knows is faulty, and he never cheats on a deal. Dangerous to cross but strictly professional.
