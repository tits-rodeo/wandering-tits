# The Spore Garden

A potions and alchemical supplies shop in the Market Ward, maintained by a consciousness that thinks in networks.

## Proprietor: The Gardener (Myconid)

*A large humanoid shape made entirely of different colored fungal fruiting bodies, connected by a network of mycelial threads. It doesn't have a face, but somehow you know it's looking at you. The shop smells of earth, decay, and something faintly medicinal.*

A myconid—a fungal colony with a hive consciousness that thinks in strange, interconnected patterns. Speaks slowly, as if each word requires consensus among its component parts. Genuinely helpful, but its perspective is utterly alien. 

Understands alchemy through fungal fermentation and spore dispersal. Knows about exotic reagents from the Underdark and the Elemental Planes. Potions are highly reliable because the Gardener is fundamentally incapable of dishonesty—it operates on a logic of balance and growth.

## Notable Policy

The Gardener views alchemy as growth and balance. It will not sell anything it deems harmful to the natural order, though its definition of "natural" is broad and includes fungal systems across all planes. All goods are verifiable—the Gardener's inability to lie is legendary.
