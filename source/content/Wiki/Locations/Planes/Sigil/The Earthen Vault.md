# The Earthen Vault

An armor shop in the Market Ward operated by a creature not of this plane.

## Proprietor: Khrak (Xorn)

*A bizarre creature: a floating, roughly spherical body made of rocky hide with three clawed limbs arranged symmetrically around it. It has three mouths, and gems and metals are visibly embedded in its hide like decorations. It moves through the shop by phasing partially through walls and floor.*

An earth elemental who amassed a collection of rare metals and ores by traveling the Elemental Plane and the Underdark. Speaks in a strange, echoing voice that sounds like grinding stone. Doesn't fully understand humanoid concepts but knows materials with an intuitive, almost geological knowledge. 

Prices metals by weight and rarity, not conventional armor values. Prefers to trade or barter rather than use gold. Negotiations are fascinating and slightly unsettling—Khrak will explain the planar origin and properties of every material in meticulous detail.

## Notable Policy

Khrak will only work with materials it deems worthy. It views each piece as having its own significance and refuses to create "ordinary" items. Customers willing to pay premium prices for genuinely unique materials will find no better source in Sigil.
