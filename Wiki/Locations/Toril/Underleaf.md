Underleaf is a small waystation town on the [[Sword Coast]]'s Trade Way, sitting where the road runs alongside the southwestern eaves of the [[Misty Forest]].

## Description
Underleaf's buildings crowd close beneath the first stretch of forest canopy, their roofs overhung with branches and threaded with hanging moss -- travelers coming up the Trade Way often see the tree line before they see the town itself. Homes are built low and wide rather than tall, and doorways, furniture, and market stalls come in a mismatched range of sizes to suit whoever's using them that season. Lanterns strung between the eaves are kept burning at all hours, a standing signal that the town is open to anyone who reaches it.

## Society
Underleaf's population is a mix of folk who, for one reason or another, drew too much attention in the towns and cities behind them -- tieflings born under the wrong omen, half-orcs from villages that never let them forget it, genasi, dragonborn wanderers, and stranger sorts besides. Nobody in Underleaf asks where you're from unless you offer it first. The town's unspoken rule is that a person's welcome depends on how they act in Underleaf, not on what they are or where they've been. Newcomers are given a few days' lodging and a meal on the house before anyone expects them to start paying their way.

Trade caravans on the Trade Way stop here to resupply, and Underleaf has built a modest livelihood selling the sort of goods only its residents can easily produce or trade for -- forest herbs, elven mead brought down from deeper in the Misty Forest, and metalwork and leatherwork from craftsfolk who couldn't get a fair price for it elsewhere.

## Government
Underleaf has no lord or council in the traditional sense. Disputes and decisions go before a rotating body of townsfolk called the Circle, chosen by lot every season, with the understanding that anyone might be asked to serve. It's a slow, informal system, but it keeps any one faction -- or any one grudge -- from taking hold of the town for long.
