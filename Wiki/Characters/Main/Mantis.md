## Backstory
Mantis is a thri-kreen warrior from another planet currently working off a debt at [[The Wandering Tit]] tavern.

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
Mantis was a gladiator renowned among the Thri-Kreen on the planet Coronatus. She was the Princess’ lover and close to the royal family. 

In order to promote the King’s son as the next ruler, Mantis was asked to lose to the Prince in a battle. Loyal to the King, Mantis accepted. However, at the climax of the battle, the Prince revealed that the plan was for him to kill Mantis, garnering more popularity for himself by winning the duel. Mantis struck back and killed the Prince in bloody fashion. 

Mantis confronted the King about the Prince’s intentions and the King revealed that it was his idea to have Mantis killed. Mantis tried to kill the King, but ultimately failed. Mantis underestimated the King, as he was much stronger than anticipated. 

Having sacrificed her pride for the kingdom by accepting the battle with the Prince, lost a battle to the King, killed her lover’s (the Princess) brother, and with the only other option being execution, Mantis fled the planet in a spelljammer thorn ship.

Mantis eventually crash landed on Toril, into [[The Wandering Tit]] in the [[Sword Coast]]. In restitution, the timber of the ship was used to rebuild the tavern, and Mantis agreed to pay off her debt by working at the tavern. 

The crystal throne, the spelljammer helm of the ship, was mostly salvageable, and kept hidden. It will need heavy repair to become functional again.

## (DM Only) Relations

## (DM Only) Goals / Motivations
- **Short-term:** Repay my debt to the tavern
- **Mid-term:** Rebuild my weapon collection
