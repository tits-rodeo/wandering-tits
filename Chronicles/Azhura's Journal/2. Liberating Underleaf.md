## 14 Eleint, 1493 DR

I'm writing from the magic shop in the early morning of the 14th of Eleint. We are safe, at least for now... at least until light comes and day breaks, but for the moment, the shadows are our refuge. Now, let me narrate the events of this past day, for they have been weighing heavily on my mind and conscience.

Smoke. That was the first thing waiting for us after escaping that damned place.

Smoke, ash, and beneath it something faintly familiar that I wish I did not recognize so easily—the scent of burned flesh.

In the distance, three figures moved along the road, all of them wearing crimson-red cloaks: a color I was far too familiar with, the colors of my family.

After everything inside the dreadnought—or temple, or whatever it truly was—I had allowed myself to believe that returning here would at least mean returning to something familiar. Instead, we had apparently escaped one nightmare only to crawl directly into another.

We had not gone far before someone approached us. It took us a moment to recognize [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]], the town's goblin mail carrier. She had been at the inn a few times, but I had never had a good opportunity to speak with her directly. At that moment, however, she looked less like someone delivering letters and more like someone who had spent the last day desperately trying not to be noticed.

She apprised us of the situation, explaining how the [[Crimson Flame]] Clan had arrived almost from nowhere and how people had seen a dragon flying over the town.

Then she said something that made my heart sink.

A woman had arrived in town.

A dragon lady.

[[Ashra]].

I could feel my heart beating faster, and my breath became ragged and shallow. I could even feel my eyes opening wide as I tried to take everything in, desperately searching for her and for somewhere to hide.

I had to start telling myself to breathe and calm down, actively focusing on keeping control of myself before the fear had a chance to overtake me.

Breathe.

Think.

Do not panic.

Do not let her see you panic.

Unfortunately, I was not entirely successful in calming myself, but I was successful enough to follow [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] and the others into the town smithy... the smithy that had belonged to [[Renn Vask|Renn]].

I tried not to think too much about that. As annoying as he had been, I still wished I could have saved him.

Once we were inside, [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] explained what had happened while we were gone.

The cultists had seized the town council and were holding its members hostage. Their threat was simple: one member of [[The Circle|the Circle]] would burn each day until the town surrendered the woman [[Ashra]] had come looking for... a dragonblood woman who lived here.

Me.

It was happening again. This was the [[Monastery of the Platinum Dawn]] all over again, except this time there were no other paladins or knights ready to put up a fight... only innocent civilians.

And it was again all my fault.

[[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] kept explaining that we had until the end of tomorrow. If I had not been surrendered to [[Ashra]] by then, according to her threat, the entire town would burn to the ground.

I wish I could believe surrender would prevent that, but I know my sister better. The moment I surrender myself to her, she will still burn everything to the ground and take whatever treasures remain for her hoard. Once a dragon raid has begun, it is very difficult to stop it.

I would know. I participated in far too many of them over the years. I told the others as much... and I also finally told them exactly who [[Ashra]] is.

My eldest sister. An adult red dragon.

I had perhaps assumed more of this was obvious than it actually was. [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] certainly had not known; the poor girl went pale enough that for a moment I thought she might faint.

That said, my companions were not particularly surprised by what I told them. Some of them were even interested in fighting the cultists—and my sister—which would have been suicidal.

Fortunately, no one was foolish enough to actually attempt it. Thanks be to the [[Bahamut|Platinum Flame]] for that.

According to [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]], [[Ashra]] spends the day within the town but withdraws at night to a camp outside it. That gave us a window, allowing us to rest until the early evening, when apparently she had left. Then we began discussing what to do next.

My first thought was evacuation. Get everyone who could move into the [[Misty Forest]]. Scatter them before [[Ashra]] realizes what is happening. If necessary, I could remain behind and draw her attention while the others escaped.

I said as much, but Mr. [[Tok Tok]], [[Quinsley]], and [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] objected immediately.

Apparently, while [[Ashra]] and the cultists had been tightening their grip on the settlement, some of the locals had begun organizing resistance from the brewery. [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] offered to guide us through a back route that was not being watched.

Once we were certain [[Ashra]] had left town, we started toward the brewery.

That was when [[Mantis]] found the tracks.

Thri-kreen. Her people.

She became tense almost immediately and said something that concerned me considerably:

"They found me."

For a moment I thought she meant my family; given recent events, I believe I can be forgiven for assuming every ominous statement somehow concerns my family. For good or ill, however, that was not the case this time. Whatever was after [[Mantis]] belonged to her past. She did not explain further, and I did not press.

I have spent months appreciating that my companions do not demand answers from me before I am prepared to give them. It would be rather hypocritical to deny [[Mantis]] the same courtesy.

The tracks followed roughly along the route we needed to take, which presented us with an unpleasant choice: we could either abandon the hidden path and risk moving closer to [[Crimson Flame]] patrols, or continue and possibly meet another Thri-kreen whose intentions we were uncertain of.

We chose to continue down the secret path, and we did not have to wonder for long whether the stranger was friendly, as something struck [[Mantis]] hard enough to tear into her thorax.

A strange curved weapon came spinning from the trees and returned toward its wielder. Then another followed. And another.

The hunter remained hidden among the branches while focusing almost entirely upon [[Mantis]]. Whatever history existed between them, it did not appear affectionate.

The fight quickly became less a battle and more a pursuit through the forest.

[[Mantis]] charged after the hunter. Mr. [[Tok Tok]] followed with considerably more speed than I expected from him. [[Trixie]] hurled those strange crimson bolts of hers between the trees, while [[Quinsley]] attempted to slow the creature with divine magic.

And then there was Jimothy.

I have not yet written about him, mostly because I am still trying to decide what Jimothy actually is. All I can say with certainty is that [[Trixie]] summoned him, and that he resembles a small woodland creature which, under different circumstances, I would have assumed had wandered too close to one of the wyvern enclosures back in my march and was about to become lunch.

Instead, he threw himself directly at the hunter and began biting pieces out of him.

Repeatedly.

The durability of the Thri-kreen continues to surprise me. Mortals though they may be, they are remarkably difficult to kill.

I had sparred with [[Mantis]] enough to know that her people were sturdy, but until then I had not understood exactly how sturdy.

The hunter endured wounds that would have left most creatures bleeding helplessly into the leaves. [[Mantis]] cut through pieces of his carapace. Mr. [[Tok Tok]] managed to break through it with his strikes. Jimothy kept finding exposed places to sink his teeth. Still, the hunter kept moving.

He retreated deeper into the trees, using his weapons to deflect attacks and trying repeatedly to put distance between himself and us.

I marked him with [[Bahamut]]'s sigil and joined the pursuit, throwing one of my javelins after him.

I missed. Badly.

I am beginning to think I need considerably more practice with the things. It was honestly rather pathetic that I could not land a single one, though fortunately no one else was standing where mine landed.

Our pursuit was not quiet, though. Branches broke. Weapons struck trees. [[Mantis]] moved through the undergrowth with all the subtlety one might expect from four-armed warriors trying to murder one another at speed.

Eventually, we heard movement nearby and saw far too late that a [[Crimson Flame]] patrol archer had come to investigate the noise.

Wonderful.

We had gone from attempting to secretly contact a resistance movement to fighting [[Mantis]]'s mysterious pursuer while one of my family's soldiers wandered directly toward us.

I attempted to salvage the situation by invoking the force of my oath and commanding the archer to ignore what he had seen and leave us.

He resisted.

Today is truly not my day.

[[Quinsley]] responded more effectively by striking him with divine light, and from that point forward, everything became confused.

The hunter tried to escape, but we pursued him relentlessly. [[Mantis]] carved through his armor whenever she could reach him. Mr. [[Tok Tok]] kept pace somehow, moving through the forest faster than I had ever seen him move, while [[Trixie]]'s magic continued eating away at the creature from a distance.

And Jimothy—

Gods preserve me.

Jimothy finally caught the hunter at a point where [[Mantis]] had already torn open the armor around his neck. The little beast buried his teeth into the exposed flesh, digging deep until the hunter stopped moving.

Whatever [[Mantis]]'s people train themselves to endure, there are apparently limits after all.

With the hunter finally dead, the rest of us were free to turn our full attention toward the [[Crimson Flame]] archer.

What followed was considerably less elegant than I would prefer to record in detail. [[Quinsley]] burned him with divine magic while [[Trixie]] continued tearing into him with those crimson bolts of hers. [[Mantis]], meanwhile—and only [[Bahamut]] knows how—arrived carrying one of the dead hunter's weapons and immediately joined the attack.

Mr. [[Tok Tok]] threw several projectiles at the man. The most spectacular of these was a cabbage. Yes. A cabbage.

He crossed an absurd distance through the trees, landed in a vegetable patch, grabbed a cabbage, and threw it directly at the cultist's head. And, astonishingly, it struck him squarely. Unfortunately, the cabbage had begun to spoil and therefore lacked the necessary structural integrity to be a particularly effective weapon. I do not know whether to be disappointed or impressed.

The archer fought desperately once he realized he was surrounded. He managed to wound [[Quinsley]] and even put an arrow into Jimothy, who immediately retreated behind my wings.

The little creature waited there until an opportunity presented itself, then launched himself from behind me and tore into the archer's throat.

That was the end of the fight: two enemies dead and our attempt at stealth considerably less successful than intended.

Fortunately, [[Ashra]] remained blissfully unaware—or at least I pray she remained unaware—that her little sister was crawling through the bushes murdering her patrols.

We dragged the archer's body into a nearby magic shop before anyone else could discover it. That was where we met [[Fen]], who immediately warned us about his doorknob. Apparently, it bites.

I am far too tired, and far too concerned about [[Ashra]], to worry about such trifling matters at present.

Another [[Crimson Flame]] archer came around the corner before we had properly hidden the evidence, as there was still far too much blood outside. [[Fen]] saw the patrolman approaching and immediately claimed that the blood belonged to him.

And... somehow, this worked.

The archer looked at the old man, looked at the blood, muttered something about old fools, and left.

So, somehow, we are still alive.

The resistance is somewhere ahead of us. The Circle remains captive. [[Mantis]]'s people, or at least one of them, have apparently found her. But perhaps more importantly...

[[Ashra]] is here.

And if she discovers I am here before we are ready—

*Several deep marks score the parchment before the sentence resumes.*

No. I will not give her that satisfaction.

Not again.

## 15 Eleint, 1493 DR

We spent the remainder of the night in [[Fen]]'s magic shop. After the battle with the thri-kreen and the archer, none of us were in any condition to continue through the town, especially with [[Ashra]] expected to return by morning.

We woke before dawn.

[[Trixie]] sent an imp to scout the area and learn more about the [[Crimson Flame]] forces occupying the town. While it searched, we prepared to reach the brewery and contact the resistance. If possible, we would help evacuate the townspeople into the forest before [[Ashra]] could carry out her threat to burn the town.

[[Fen]] offered us several enchanted items to use. I took the [[Boots of Elvenkind]], which helped make my footsteps quieter. I figured I could use the help with my stealth, since my armor and body are difficult not to notice.

We somehow avoided the patrols and made our way toward the brewery. [[Quinsley]] nearly drew the attention of one of the guards, but [[Trixie]] distracted him with her imp while I used [[Amulet of Bahamut|my amulet]] to change my clothing into something better suited to the darkness.

After some effort, we finally reached the brewery and entered through a window at the back. [[Brynn Thatcher|Brynn]], its owner, found us there and led us into the basement.

I had high hopes that the people of the town—this resistance—would be of significant help in fighting off my former clan.

I was sorely disappointed. The resistance was smaller than I had imagined.

There was [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]], the owner of the grocery store, and only one other person. Certainly not enough to fight the [[Crimson Flame]], much less [[Ashra]].

[[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]] confronted us about [[Garrek]], his adopted son, who had died aboard the dreadnought while we survived. [[Tobin]] spoke about the people we had lost, and his words affected everyone in the room, especially [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]]. He left the basement, carrying his grief and anger with him.

I could barely contain my own guilt; their deaths were on me. I may not have caused every danger we faced, but I brought that danger to their door. I will not allow more people to die for my sake if I can prevent it.

I went after [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]]. The least I could do was take responsibility and buy the villagers some time to evacuate. However, [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]] came up with a different plan instead.

He explained that the town had once been occupied by the [[Verdant Claw]], an organization connected to the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and supported by a green dragon, Chuth. The resistance had survived that occupation by using tunnels beneath the town to move unseen and strike from unexpected places.

According to [[Fen]], the [[Verdant Claw]] had been working with the [[Cult of the Dragon|Cult]] in an attempt to bring [[Tiamat]] out of [[Avernus]]. I found it fascinating that his account resembled the stories [[Skaerth|Father]] used to tell us, though [[Skaerth|Father]] naturally placed more emphasis on dragons and the failures of mortals.

In the end, we agreed to retrieve an arcane focus belonging to the town's former hero: a shield that had helped save the settlement from the green dragon. Afterward, we would attempt to rescue the last remaining kidnapped member of [[The Circle|the Circle]], a  [[Vantha Emberlight|dragonborn woman]] who had served as one of the town's leaders.

The tunnels led us to a chamber filled with old beds and a statue of an angelic warrior. The statue carried the shield, but it also wore a helmet and pauldron that seemed to possess a divine connection to [[Helm]].

[[Quinsley]] put on the helmet.

She was immediately overcome by a vision. Darkness gathered around her, and she cried out the name [[Neronvain]]. A shadowy figure appeared, wearing a distorted, draconic helmet.

I remember little of the battle beyond the final strike. I lunged from one of the beds, nearly lost my balance, and recovered with the help of my wings and tail. I brought my sword around in a backswing and let the power of the [[Bahamut|Platinum Flame]] burn through the blade.

The apparition dissolved into dark smoke.

Afterward, we searched the room, where I found a small wooden bird.

*Some fast, passing scribbles, clearly Azhura's writing but at the same time distinct from her normal calligraphy and style: **Gold! There was gold, my hoard, I needed it, it... it calls to me!***

I lost control.

[[Quinsley]] found a pouch of gold, and the sight of the coins awakened something in me, something old and instinctive. I tried to take them from her. When she said she was not trying to hoard them, memories of my own hoard came rushing back: my lair, my servants, my territory, and everything that had been taken from me!

Thanks be to [[Tok Tok|Mr. Tok Tok]], who used some kind of spell to bring me back to myself. Otherwise, I do not want to imagine what could have happened.

I am so ashamed. I do not know how else to express the disappointment I feel toward myself. I thought the curse had taken nearly everything from me that made me a dragon, but apparently, it left more behind than I realized.

[[Quinsley]] had done nothing wrong, and yet I had frightened her over a handful of coins. We reconciled, but I cannot pretend the incident did not trouble me.

We continued through the tunnels, and took a beat to surface near the burned remains of the [[The Wandering Tit|Wandering Tit]]. There, we noticed a faint, sweet smell drifting from the direction of the cemetery. We left it unexplored for the moment, returning to the tunnels and leaving the shield to [[Fen|the mage]] before continuing toward the captive member of [[The Circle|the Circle]].

Not long afterward, we heard fighting.

In a large chamber where several corridors met, we found [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]], [[Tobin]], and [[Brynn Thatcher|Brynn]] fighting six creatures that looked like oversized mixtures of bats and mosquitoes. We joined the battle and managed to defeat them before anyone was killed.

While searching the remains, [[Trixie]] found another pouch of gold, and the same instinct rose in me again. However, I was somehow able to recognize it before I lost control.

I dug my claws into myself until the pain cleared my thoughts. It worked. I did not attack her. It is a small victory, but I will take it.

[[Ashra]] may return at any moment. The town remains occupied, the last member of [[The Circle|the Circle]] is still captive, and the resistance is preparing to move against forces far greater than itself.

After dealing with the creatures in the infirmary, we continued exploring the tunnels beneath the town. [[Trixie]] sent her imp ahead through the northern passage. The creature eventually found its way toward the central plaza before returning to us, which at least gave us some idea of where the tunnels connected to the surface.

The rest of us tried the northwestern passage and eventually reached the cemetery. However, we did not stay long, as there was already a [[Crimson Flame]] patrol moving through the area and starting another fight simply to investigate a cemetery seemed unnecessarily reckless.

So we withdrew back toward the infirmary instead. There, we encountered [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]], who had explored the western tunnel while we were gone. According to her, it led to [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]]'s home, leaving only the northern branch unexplored. Naturally, that one was partially collapsed, so we began clearing the cave-in by hand.

I would like to take this opportunity to state that I miss kobolds. Deeply.

There are many things about my former life I do not miss, but having an entire household of small, industrious creatures available whenever a tunnel needed excavation was apparently a luxury I had never properly appreciated.

We had been digging for some time when one of the wooden supports gave way. I heard the timber crack immediately before part of the ceiling came down on top of me.

It hurt.

Quite a lot.

Fortunately, nothing important appeared to be broken, and after some assistance from the others. and several decidedly impolite words from me, we continued until we finally forced our way through. What waited on the other side almost made the effort worthwhile.

Almost.

The chamber beyond had once been some kind of armory. Weapons and pieces of armor were piled everywhere, though most had deteriorated badly. Rust had eaten through blades and buckles, leather had rotted away, and several suits of armor looked as though they would collapse if someone breathed too forcefully in their direction. 

A few pieces, however, had survived in remarkably good condition. [[Dragon Slayer Weapon|Two shortswords, a longsword, and a mace]] were completely untouched by the decay surrounding them. Each carried a faint magical glow.

We also found a dragon tooth, though I could not identify what kind of dragon it had come from. That troubled me more than I care to admit.

Then we found the book... A hand-copied tome titled "[[The Compleat Dragon-Hunter]]." 

Of course.

Because apparently this town has hidden underground armories containing manuals dedicated to murdering my kind. I suppose I should be offended. On the other hand, considering [[Ashra]] is currently occupying the settlement, I cannot deny that the timing is rather convenient.

The book contained descriptions of techniques intended specifically for fighting dragons. Some of it was familiar enough: weak angles, ways of surviving breath attacks, methods of exploiting the limitations of wings and larger bodies. 

But there were also sections I did not immediately understand. One page in particular contained a collection of strange symbols. 

I stared at them for some time. Nothing. No language I recognized. No magical notation that made sense to me.

We decided to rest for a short while before continuing. Time remains precious, but between the shadow in the old barracks, those disgusting parasites in the infirmary, the tunnel creatures, and now excavating half a collapsed passage by hand, even we needed a moment.

*A hurried note has been inserted between paragraphs.*

[[The Dragonbane Refrain|IT WAS MUSIC]].

The symbols were MUSIC.

[[Trixie]] looked at them for what seemed like five seconds and immediately began playing the damned thing. I am deeply embarrassed.

*The handwriting returns to its usual style.*

The melody itself was strange. Whatever its purpose, after hearing it I felt noticeably better prepared for the possibility of facing a dragon. [[Trixie]] seemed similarly certain that it would help us resist some of the effects such creatures can bring to bear.

I will refrain from commenting upon the indignity of benefiting from a dragon-hunting manual. For now.

After resting, we began debating where to go next until [[Tok Tok|Mr. Tok Tok]] reminded us of something considerably more important than my wounded pride: the dragonborn woman. The final kidnapped member of [[The Circle|the Circle]] was being held in the administrative center.

We returned to the infirmary and spoke with [[Iris Bellwether|Iris]] again. She explained that [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]]'s house stood beside the administrative building, which meant the western tunnel she had explored earlier could bring us almost directly to our destination.

For once, something worked in our favor. We followed the passage to the house and slipped out through the rear since our intention was to cross the remaining distance unseen. However, we didn't quite succeed as [[Trixie]] spotted an archer before we reached the building... and unfortunately, the archer also spotted us.

She reacted immediately, striking him with one of her blasts and knocking him from the tree before he could raise a proper alarm. Another guard nearby noticed the commotion, but Mr. [[Tok Tok]] somehow convinced him that the archer had merely gone somewhere inappropriate to relieve himself.

I do not understand how Mr. [[Tok Tok]] accomplishes these things; I have decided it may be better not to ask.

Anyway, we reached one of the administrative building's windows. I managed to pry it open and began climbing through. I almost lost my balance... again. Fortunately, my tail continues to prove more useful than I gave it credit for. I caught the upper edge of the window with it, stopped myself from falling, and managed to pull myself back into position.

Inside the room was a sleeping archer. We attempted to make certain he never had the opportunity to raise an alarm.

That did not go according to plan.

Despite [[Quinsley]], [[Trixie]], and me striking him almost simultaneously, the man somehow remained alive and immediately tried to escape. [[Trixie]]'s claws had damaged his throat badly enough that his attempt to scream became little more than a horrible gurgling noise, but it still alerted an armored thug farther down the corridor.

[[Mantis]] caught the archer before he escaped, while the rest of us dealt with the thug; unfortunately, the thug managed one final scream before we brought him down. 

So much for stealth. The sound carried through the building, and within moments [[Ashra]]'s fanatics began appearing from the adjoining rooms.

One of them recognized me immediately.

I find my sister's mortal followers deeply unsettling. They seem convinced that faithful service to [[Ashra]] will somehow allow them to become dragons themselves. Whether my sister actually promises them this or merely encourages the belief, I do not know. Either way, they imitate all the worst things about us while apparently aspiring to become even worse. [[Ashra]]'s followers may be the most insufferable people I have encountered outside of [[Infernia]]'s.

Then a second fanatic burst into the corridor, took one look at [[Mantis]], and screamed: "By the gods! It is a pink roach!" 

I have heard many terrible battle cries, but that has to be the stupidest.

She followed the insult with a blast of sacred flame powerful enough to drive [[Mantis]] to one knee, which made the situation considerably less amusing. 

That was apparently all the encouragement [[Mantis]] required. Between her, Mr. [[Tok Tok]], and [[Trixie]], the second fanatic did not remain a problem for very long.

The first, meanwhile, decided survival was preferable to fanaticism and fled into what appeared to be a pigeon room, locking the door behind her.

I went after her and found that the door was barred, so I lowered my shoulder and hit it with everything I had. The door flew inward and struck the fanatic directly in the face. She staggered backward with a bloody nose, at which point [[Quinsley]] stepped through the doorway and struck her across the back of the head with her mace, making her collapse.

Alive. Useful.

For all the noise and chaos, the important result was simple: three of [[Ashra]]'s people were dead, one fanatic was alive in our custody, and the administrative center was temporarily ours.

With the fighting finally over, I began checking the remaining rooms.

That was when I entered the conference chamber and saw the long table, the scattered documents, and the tea service arranged near the seat at its head. At first it was simply another room being used by [[Ashra]]'s people, until I looked more closely at the cup beside the pot.

I stopped moving. I knew that cup. Not one like it... that one. [[Ashra]]'s.

For a moment the rest of the building seemed to disappear around me. I remembered standing beside my sister while she sat comfortably at a table, ordering me to pour for her as though that were the most natural thing in the world.

Whenever [[Skaerth|Father]] confined me, [[Ashra]] seemed to consider it an opportunity of her own. She would have me pulled from my chains, dress me as a servant, and force me to wait upon her like some mortal maid she had purchased for her amusement.

There was never a correct way to serve her. If the tea was too hot, I was punished; if it was too cold, I was punished. Sometimes nothing was wrong with it at all and she would still pour it over my head simply because she disliked the taste. Or perhaps because she wanted to remind me that there was nothing I could do about it.

Looking at the cup brought back another memory as well, one I had tried very hard not to dwell upon. It had been during one of the early banquets of the drow negotiations, when [[Ashra]] offered me her favorite cup. 

I remember how surprised I was. For the briefest moment, like an idiot, I thought she was trying to be kind to me. I thought perhaps she had finally decided to treat me like a sister. Perhaps, for once, I might be allowed to sit beside her and [[Infernia]] instead of beneath them. So I drank.

Whatever [[Ashra]] had put into the tea was barely less than poison. The taste struck me first, followed immediately by the burning, and I spat it across the table before I could stop myself. Across the banquet. Across the negotiations [[Skaerth|Father]] had been trying to conduct.

[[Ashra]] laughed. I remember the expressions of the drow seated around us, but [[Skaerth|Father]]'s face is the one that stayed with me. He did not find it amusing.

*Several words have been blurred by water across the page.*

When we returned home, [[Skaerth|Father]] locked me away again. I remember the chains and the dungeon, and then [[Lord Halvern|Halvern]] arriving to administer the months of correction [[Skaerth|Father]] believed I had earned because I had embarrassed him.

[[Ashra]] had humiliated me, but somehow I was the one who had behaved improperly.

[[Lord Halvern]] was sent to re-educate me. A mortal. A piece of scum like him had been given authority to—

*The next several words are similarly illegible beneath water stains.*

I do not remember when I left the conference room. The next thing I remember clearly is sitting on the floor against one of the walls, my knees pulled against my chest while I cried so hard that I could barely breathe. [[Mantis]] and Mr. [[Tok Tok]] were already beside me, though I do not know how long they had been there or how long they had been trying to get me to respond.

Eventually [[Mantis]] sat down beside me and wrapped her arms around me. I leaned into her without resisting. I did not pretend I was fine, and for once I did not try to convince myself that needing someone beside me was weakness.

My crying eventually drew [[Quinsley]] out as well, and the worry on her face made it clear how badly I must have frightened them. All of them stayed with me until my breathing finally steadied and I could recognize the room around me again for what it was: the administrative center beneath an occupied mortal town, not [[Skaerth|Father]]'s dungeon.

Eventually the teacup became only a teacup again. Once I could breathe without shaking, I wiped my eyes and assured everyone that I was all right, though judging by their expressions I do not think any of them believed me. I am not entirely certain I believed it myself, but we could not remain sitting there forever, and there was still one room left to search.

The final door was heavy iron, with a strange symbol worked into what appeared to be its locking mechanism. We could not immediately open it, but there was a small viewing panel set into the door, and when we pulled it aside we discovered that the room beyond was not empty. A dragonborn woman was chained inside.

[[Vantha Emberlight]], the final captive member of [[The Circle]].

She had clearly been there for some time. Her captors had beaten her badly, leaving bruises and wounds across her body before chaining her inside the room, and seeing her in that condition made whatever lingering weakness I still felt from my breakdown seem considerably less important.

Whatever strength I had left, I used it to heal her. I placed my hands upon her and gave the last of [[Bahamut]]'s restorative power I could call upon, closing some of the wounds and easing the worst of the bruising.

There were weapons and armor stored inside the room as well, along with a large iron chest against one wall. I took the plate armor and greatsword from one of the dead thugs while we worked to free her.

Then Miss [[Vantha Emberlight|Emberlight]] looked properly at me... and recoiled.

I cannot blame her. A draconic woman covered in red scales, standing in the middle of a building occupied by the [[Crimson Flame]], while [[Ashra]] herself terrorizes the town.

Of course she was afraid... so... I stepped away... I did the only thing I could... I lowered my head and left the room letting [[Mantis]] take my place.

She and Mr. [[Tok Tok]] helped [[Vantha Emberlight|Vantha]] from her chains while [[Quinsley]] and I carried the unconscious fanatic back toward the tunnels. Once we returned to the infirmary, [[Trixie]] tied our prisoner securely to one of the beds and gagged her, and eventually the rest of us gathered there again with [[Vantha Emberlight|Vantha]] safely out of [[Ashra]]'s hands.

It was only then, with everyone together and no one actively trying to kill us for perhaps the first time in what felt like far too long, that I had a chance to consider what we had actually accomplished. [[The Circle]]'s final captive was free, three of [[Ashra]]'s people were dead, one of her fanatics was now our prisoner, and for the moment at least, her forces had lost control of the administrative center.

Under almost any other circumstances, I might have considered that a victory. The problem, of course, is [[Ashra]].

Somewhere above us my sister either already knows what happened or will discover it very soon. If she has not yet returned to town, she will, and when she does she will find the prisoner she intended to use as leverage gone, several of her servants dead, and one of her own fanatics missing.
