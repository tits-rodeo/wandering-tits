# Fork Off

A modest storefront in the Market Ward of Sigil. The sign is plain, with tiny lettering underneath indicating: "Tuning Forks & Planar Goods."

## Interior

Inside, shelves display tuning forks in dozens of styles and materials, each labeled by plane. The shop also stocks a limited but carefully curated selection of magical artifacts useful for planar travel: protective amulets, scrolls of planeshift, gear designed for overcoming specific planar hazards.

## Proprietor: Damroodleboss Delimimus

*Damroodleboss Delimimus is a gnome, barely three feet tall, but his presence is unsettling. Wild hair rises from his head like dark tentacles. A cloak covered in spiderweb patterns hangs from his shoulders. One monocle sits normally over his eye, while a second lens is mounted on a thin armature attached to his headband, like a jeweler's loupe. His staff is a massive tuning fork with a half-melted candle wedged between the prongs. Several smaller tuning forks orbit slowly around his head of their own accord, and more hang from a necklace around his neck. Everything about him suggests a connection to something far stranger than Sigil.*

Boss is grumpy and direct, but runs a reliable operation. He knows the locations of various establishments, contacts, and services throughout Sigil.

## Services

- **Tuning Fork Sales**: Specialized tuning forks keyed to specific planes and gates
- **Planar Artifacts**: Curated selection of items useful for planar travel
- **Information Brokerage**: Boss provides information on locations, establishments, and services in Sigil for a price
