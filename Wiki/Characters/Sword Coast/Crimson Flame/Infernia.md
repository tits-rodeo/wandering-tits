She is about as old as [[Azhura]], she was always competing with Azhura and there is a significant rivalrly between the two. Just as Azhura is close to [[Valraxis]], Infernia is close to Ashra. Daughter of Skaerth and [[Pyravyl]], sixty years Azhura's elder despite the closeness in temperament. Received a share of Azhura's confiscated hoard after her escape.

## (DM Only) Character

A red dragon and high noble of the [[Crimson Flame]], Infernia is the family's blunt instrument where [[Ashra]] is its scalpel. Where Ashra works through court maneuvering and calculated humiliation, Infernia proves herself through direct confrontation, glory-seeking, and open force, and she was Azhura's longstanding childhood rival and bully well before Ashra's own campaign against her began. She killed Sir Garrick during the destruction of the [[Monastery of the Platinum Dawn]], one of the clearest marks of how far she's willing to go to settle a grudge or prove herself in a fight.

She and Ashra are closely allied, and Infernia backs her sister's plays without much need for persuading, including supporting Ashra's demand that Azhura perform the Dance of Iron and Flame during the Feast Below. That alliance has limits rooted in family possessiveness rather than principle, though: when deathless envoys at the Feast Below assessed Azhura as prey and ritual material, Infernia was genuinely offended. She can torment Azhura herself, as a sister asserting her place in the family hierarchy, but she doesn't tolerate an outsider laying claim to what she still considers Skaerth's daughter.

She carries herself with an arrogance that reads as superiority toward anyone she judges weaker, and treats confrontation itself as proof of status rather than a means to an end. Her exact territory and formal title within the Crimson Flame are unclear, as is any role she may have played in the Deep Gate intelligence leak or the delayed reinforcements during its siege.
