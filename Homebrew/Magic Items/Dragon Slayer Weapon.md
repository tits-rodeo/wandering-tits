**Magic Item**
_(Weapon (any Simple or Martial), Rare)_
- +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.
- On a hit against a Dragon, the target takes an extra **3d6** damage of the weapon's type.

## (DM Only) Origin
- Underfoot-era. Found in Maud's Resistance Cache: 2 shortswords, 1 longsword, 1 mace.
