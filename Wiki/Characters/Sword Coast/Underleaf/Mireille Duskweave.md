## Background
A harengon fortune teller who lives out of a parked carriage near Underleaf's cemetery, long ears in constant motion, wrapped in mismatched shawls. Nobody in town remembers her arriving, and the carriage hasn't moved since. Wary of outsiders and the town's own occupiers alike.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Luna Lovegood (*Harry Potter*) energy — dreamy, off in her own world, says unsettling true things everyone else dismisses as weird. For a harengon, that same ever-alert, twitchy quality prey animals have reads as spaciness rather than nervousness — she's not missing what's around her, she's just paying attention to something else at the same time.
- **Voice:** Soft, unhurried, drifts mid-sentence into observations that sound like non sequiturs until they turn out not to be.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** Her carriage sits close enough to the cemetery that whatever she's been "seeing" out there lately, she hasn't been wrong yet.
- **Home:** A carriage, parked near the cemetery.
- **Purpose:** In *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]*, wary of both the occupiers and the party at first — earning her trust nets a reading with real tactical value for the finale at Hearth Hall.
