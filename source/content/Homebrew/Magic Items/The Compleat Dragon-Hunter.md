**Magic Item**
_(Book, mundane)_
- Hand-copied dragon-hunting manual. Weak angles, surviving breath weapons, exploiting wings and bulk.
- **Study (15 min, can be done during a Short or Long Rest):** Advantage on attack rolls against dragons for 24 hours.
	- One reader at a time. Max 4 readers per Short Rest; each extra reader adds 15 min to the rest.
- **Reference:** Advantage on Intelligence checks to recall dragon lore or tactics while consulting it.
- **Sheet music:** Source of [[The Dragonbane Refrain]]. Reads as meaningless symbols to non-musicians.

## (DM Only) Origin
- Copied at Candlekeep by a member of the original Underfoot resistance. Found in Maud's Resistance Cache.
