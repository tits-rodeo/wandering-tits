## Background
An enormous, gentle half-orc who carried a battered lute case he could never quite learn to play. Stockboy at the local grocer and regular at [[The Wandering Tit]], well-liked despite his size scaring off strangers. [[Oswin Marrow]], Underleaf's grocer, took him in years ago as an apprentice and, in every way that mattered, a son — gave him work and a room in the back of the shop.

He was dragged into the donjon along with the other patrons during the [[Crimson Flame]] attack on the tavern (see *[[Adventure - Into the Gullet|Into the Gullet]]*). He died there, struck down while carrying the still-unconscious [[Trixie]] to safety — he never made it out of the corridor.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Hagrid — huge, soft-spoken, kind to a fault, everyone assumes he's dangerous until he proves otherwise.
- **Voice:** Low, slow, gentle. Apologizes for taking up space.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** Was trying to work up the nerve to ask [[Trixie]] for lute lessons the night the tavern burned.
- **Purpose:** A big, sympathetic presence whose death makes his fate feel disproportionately cruel. In *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]*, [[Oswin Marrow|Oswin]]'s grief over him is the emotional core of the party earning the resistance's trust.
