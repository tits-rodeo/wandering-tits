A song written for [[Trixie]] by [[Garrek]] before his untimely passing. He never had a chance to present it to her.

**Verse 1**
Spotted tabaxi lazin' on the bar,
Ain't got a copper and I've wandered too far.
I play all night, I sleep all day,
I swipe your gold and I slink away.

**Chorus**
Tabaxi strut, I'm a tavern cat,
I'm a one-cat orchestra, hey that's that.
Get a mug thrown at me from a drunk old man,
Pry my dinner off the kitchen pan.

*Meow! Don't touch my lute.*

**Verse 2**
I don't bother sweepin' floors around,
I hop up on a table when the crowd's just right,
Pluckin' up a jig on a hot summer night,
Horns and fiddles that ain't even nearby.
Sly little kitty with a glint in her eye,
They think I'm sleepy and they think I'm mild,
But I got cat class and I got cat style.
