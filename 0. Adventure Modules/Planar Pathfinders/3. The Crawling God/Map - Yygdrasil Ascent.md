# The Corrupted Ascent: A 5-Room Dungeon

## Background

The party pursues an Infected Owlbear up the diseased trunk of Yggdrasil in the Beastlands. This World Tree stretches miles into the sky with a trunk nearly a mile wide. The creature flees toward its den at the source of the corruption, using its simple cunning to create hazards along the way.

---

## 1. The Circle of Stones

> _This moss-covered platform juts from Yggdrasil's trunk about 200 feet above ground, where seven ancient standing stones form a sacred circle. The owlbear's heavy landing knocked several stones askew and gouged deep claw marks through the moss as it scrambled upward. The roots leading upward are similarly clawed, weeping so much sap that further ascent will be almost impossible._

##### Features:
- The stone circle used to help in aligning gravity for the tree in a way most advantageous for ascending it. Without, the sap makes ascent nearly impossible.
- Seven standing stones carved with glowing druidic runes, several knocked out of alignment
- Ancient druidic tools scattered around the platform's edge
- Thick anchor points on nearby branches where climbing ropes could be secured
- Central altar stone with embedded focus crystals that flicker weakly

###### Mechanics:
- **Athletics DC 18:** Each round if attempting to climb roots without fixing stones.
- **Athletics/Acrobatics DC 10:** Each round, mortai wind gusts try to push party off the platform.
- **Arcana DC 10:** To determine the original purpose of the stones.
	- Focus crystals in the center stone must be carefully adjusted, or risk magical feedback (1d6 electrical damage).
	- **Arcana DC 14:** Detect Magic to identify proper stone alignment pattern.
	- **Sleight of Hand DC 15:** Adjust focus crystals with Mage Hand without triggering magical feedback.
	- Success grants Blessing of Sure Footing (advantage on climbing checks for 1 hour). Gravity reorients toward the roots properly, allowing walking instead of climbing.
- **Athletics DC 19:** Lift and reposition four massive stones (nearly a ton each) into proper alignment
- **Woodcarver's Tools:** Can craft precise wooden supports and wedges from scattered materials to give advantage to climbing.
- **Acrobatics DC 10:** To walk up roots once magic stones are fixed.

## 2. The Ancient Observatory

> _The primary root that should continue upward appears to have been destroyed. A remarkable structure sits on a broad grassy outcrop. 
> The lower half of the structure features fitted stone blocks arranged in a perfect circle, while the upper wooden section showcases organic architecture that seems to grow from the tree itself. 
> The owlbear's rampage is evident in massive claw marks scoring the stone foundation and smashed celestial instruments scattered across the floor._

**Inside**
> *The observatory's interior reveals a fascinating blend of astronomical precision and magical craftsmanship. Star charts cover the walls alongside intricate brass instruments, while a magnificent telescope dominates the center of the room beneath an open dome. Crystalline lenses and mirrors catch the ethereal light filtering down from above, casting prismatic patterns across scattered calculation tables covered in complex mathematical notations. The owlbear's rampage has left astrolabes overturned, delicate measuring devices smashed, and several wooden support beams cracked and groaning under the structure's shifting weight.*

**Features:**

- Top of the observatory rejoins the other half of the destroyed root structure.
- Bronze-and-crystal telescope pointing skyward through the roof
- Scattered astronomical instruments and star charts across tilting floors
- Overheating magical components that spark and smoke
- **Perception DC 10:** Wooden support beams showing stress fractures, estimate 1 minute left before collapse.

###### Mechanics:
- **Woodcarver's Tools:** To craft makeshift supports and shoots stabilizing pins into rotating mechanisms
- **Attack Rolls vs AC 18:** To precisely targets mechanical lock points (requires 3 hits)
- **Sleight of Hand DC 17:** Arcane Trickster realigns three delicate astrolabe settings with Mage Hand
- **Athletics DC 15:** Werewolf holds central pillar steady while others climb. Also add 
- Time limit of 10 rounds before total collapse.
- Success stabilizes structure and telescope reveals safe climbing paths.

## The Ranger's Roost

> _This wooden platform represents masterful ranger craftsmanship, built seamlessly into Yggdrasil's bark at the tree's center point. Multiple levels connected by spiral stairs create a network of vantage points, with lean-to shelters and equipment stations scattered throughout. The owlbear's desperate thrashing damaged rope bridges and scattered supplies, while infected sap dripping from its claws has begun corrupting patches of the previously pristine wood._

**Features:**

- Owlbear is hiding here! Emerges when detected, forcing tactical combat across multiple platform levels.
- Damaged rope bridges hanging at dangerous angles between platforms
- Scattered ranger supplies and crafting materials
- Infected wood patches that pulse with dark energy
- Weapon racks and tool stations, some still intact
- Safety nets woven from branches 30 feet below main platform

###### Mechanics:
- **Woodcarver's Tools:** Craft temporary rope bridges using scattered materials
- **Sleight of Hand DC 16:** Arcane Trickster disarms weakened support rope "traps" using stealth and magic
- **Destroy Water or Create Water:** Cleanses infected sap from wooden surfaces
- **Nature DC 14:** Identify structural stress points
- **Athletics DC 15:** Werewolf catches and holds collapsing platforms while others work
- **Acrobatics DC 13:** Spending rounds on unstable platforms or fall 20 feet to safety nets (1d6 damage)

## The Floating Sanctuary

> _This impossible sight defies natural law—a small island of earth and grass floating beside Yggdrasil, tethered by an elegant rope bridge. The island hosts a cottage with crystalline windows and floating arcane crystals that orbit its perimeter. The owlbear's clumsy landing shattered several crystals and gouged deep furrows in the earth, while infected sap from its claws has begun destabilizing the levitation magic._

**Features:**

- Floating crystals in unstable orbits around the island's edge
- Wizard's cottage with arcane workshop and precision tools
- Corrupted crystal fragments that spark with dangerous energy
- Tilting platform that shifts toward Yggdrasil's trunk
- Magical herb garden with valuable components

###### Mechanics:
- **Jeweler's Tools DC 16:** Ranged Fighter repairs crystal housings and crafts stabilizing components
- **Attack Rolls vs AC 15:** Ranged Fighter shoots corrupted crystal fragments to safely shatter them
- **Arcana DC 15:** Arcane Trickster uses Detect Magic to identify salvageable vs. corrupted crystals
- **Sleight of Hand DC 17:** Arcane Trickster removes unstable crystals with Mage Hand, uses Misty Step for positioning
- **Arcana DC 14:** Aquatic Wizard reads magical "currents" maintaining levitation through water-based divination
- **Athletics DC 19:** Werewolf acts as counterweight, wrestling the tilting island level for 3 rounds
- Progressive tilting: 15° (DC 12 Dex), 30° (DC 15 Dex), near-vertical (DC 18 Dex)
- Success requires stabilizing 4 of 6 crystal anchor points to stop collapse

## The Bleeding Den

> _The party enters through a gaping wound in Yggdrasil's trunk, a vertical shaft stretching 80 feet from bottom to top with walls weeping crimson sap. Massive infected mushrooms ring the shaft at various heights, their caps glowing with sickly bioluminescence and providing the only stable platforms. At the bottom churns a deep pool of infected red sap where something vast and alien moves in the depths, while at the top the cornered owlbear clings to infected flesh growths, ready for its final desperate stand._

**Features:**

- Eight large mushroom platforms at heights of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, and 75 feet
- Deadly aboleth sap pool at bottom (certain death—4d6 acid damage per turn plus madness)
- Weeping walls that make climbing extremely treacherous
- Infected flesh growths the owlbear can merge with
- Occasional glimpses of massive tentacles in the sap pool below

###### Mechanics:

- **Wisdom Save DC 14:** All creatures resist aboleth's psychic influence at initiative count 20 or become frightened
- **Athletics DC 18:** Climbing sap-slicked walls without magical aid
- **Constitution Save DC 15:** Destroyed mushrooms release poisonous spores (1d6 poison damage, poisoned 1 minute)
- Mushroom platforms collapse after taking 15 damage (1 round warning, 2 rounds to fall)
- **Dexterity Save DC 15:** Each round, 1d4 random squares suffer acid sap drops (1d6 acid damage)
- **Wisdom Save DC 16:** Natural 1s on attacks/saves trigger hallucinations (attack nearest creature including allies)
- **Constitution Save DC 14:** Taking necrotic damage in chamber causes one level of exhaustion
- Owlbear uses vertical terrain, Flesh Merge tactics, and attempts to knock characters into deadly pool
- Victory causes aboleth to retreat deeper, allowing safe escape via Yggdrasil's magical assistance

**Final Treasure:**

- Owlbear's nest: +1 Shield, Ring of Protection, Cloak of Resistance, 400 gp
- Heart of Yggdrasil: Rare magical fruit granting permanent +1 Constitution (maximum 20)

## Environmental Safeguards

Given Yggdrasil's massive scale, the World Tree provides multiple fall protection systems:

- Branch networks every 100-200 feet (DC 15 Dexterity save to catch)
- Sap streams that slow falls like natural slides (half damage with DC 14 Dexterity save)
- Yggdrasil's Mercy: Fall damage that would kill instead leaves character at 1 HP, unconscious in branches
- Ancient druidic wards grant advantage on death saving throws while on the tree
