A resistance cell that rose in [[Underleaf]] generations ago, when the town still went by its old name, [[Underleaf|Wreathwood]]. Named for what it was: townsfolk with no army, no walls, and no standing worth the name, fighting from beneath notice rather than in the open. The town renamed itself after them once the fight was won, and it's been Underleaf ever since.

## History
Wreathwood was held for a time by **the Verdant Claw**, a cell of the [[Cult of the Dragon]] led by [[Neronvain]], the exiled elven prince of the [[Misty Forest]], newly cast out by his father [[Melandrach|King Melandrach]] and freshly risen through the Cult's ranks alongside his dragon companion, [[Chuth]]. Long before he was ever named a Wyrmspeaker, this was where he first tested what he could take and hold.

Wreathwood's mayor met the occupation with open supplication, cooperating, appeasing, hoping compliance would be mistaken for no threat at all. It didn't buy the town anything. The Verdant Claw executed him publicly regardless, in full view of the town he'd tried to bargain for. It was the proof, delivered too late for him, that survives him anyway: giving a fascist what he wants doesn't end the demands, it just teaches him there's more to be had.

That execution is what turned bystanders into the Underfoot. [[Old Maud]] was among its founders. Decades before she was ever the soft-spoken fixture of [[The Wandering Tit]] the party knew, she helped dig the first tunnels under Wreathwood and fought to take her town back. [[Oswin Marrow]] was a child during the occupation, kept safe underground while the Underfoot worked above and below him. It's precisely why he, and almost no one else left in Underleaf, understands what the town is actually facing now.

## (DM Only) The Tunnels
The tunnel between the Brewery's hidden hatch and the Grocer's House cellar (see *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]*) is a surviving fragment of a much larger network the Underfoot dug beneath Wreathwood: passages meant to move people and supplies under the Verdant Claw's notice, sheltering children like Oswin while the fighting happened elsewhere. Most of the network has long since collapsed or been built over, but enough survives, unmapped and forgotten by everyone except Oswin, to let the party move through occupied Underleaf the same way the Underfoot once did.

## (DM Only) Quinsley's Helm
The cursed ceremonial helm from Quinsley's "Haunted Helm" hook is relocated here rather than the Wandering Tit's cellar, found deeper in the tunnel network instead of the tavern. Its trapped memory isn't an unrelated shadowy cult: it's the Underfoot's own last stand against the Verdant Claw, seen through the eyes of whichever fighter carried the helm to their death. Quinsley recognizing it as a piece of her old celestial order's regalia is still the personal hook. What she walks into here is Underleaf's own buried history, not a stranger's.

Its last wearer wasn't a Wreathwood local at all: [[Tobias Keth]], a traveling Aasimar of Helm's order, passing through, who threw in with the Underfoot and died fighting alongside them in the final push against the Verdant Claw. Wreathwood had no particular tie to Helm before or after. Tobias was a stranger who chose to bleed for a town that wasn't his, which is the whole point, and part of why it resonates for Quinsley specifically. He's buried in the Cemetery among the Underfoot's other dead, an unmarked or unremarked grave rather than anything shrine-like.
