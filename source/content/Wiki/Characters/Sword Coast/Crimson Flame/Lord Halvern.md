Member of the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and minion of [[Skaerth]], he is somewhat obsessed with making [[Azhura]] his since it would be a way for him to get into the clan properly, not that the other dragons care about that but he makes for a useful minion to their schemes. He has no idea the dragons see him as nothing more than that — a useful tool, never a real path into the clan.

After the failed Feast Below summit, Skaerth confined Azhura collared and chained in the chamber that once held [[Cinder]], and put Halvern in charge of her "correction and training." He avoided leaving permanent injury, but left her sore, sleep-deprived, and increasingly terrified of commands, footsteps, and closed doors. This confinement predates her curse and is separate from her later imprisonment. Valraxis arranged his reassignment to a dangerous northern garrison after the Siege of the Deep Gate.
