## Background
A weathered caravan master who handled Underleaf's Trade Way logistics and negotiations with passing merchants — more organizer than orator, but kept deals fair and caravans moving. Sat on Underleaf's Circle, the town's rotating governing body.

When [[Ashra]] and the occupying [[Crimson Flame]] took the town, her ultimatum was simple: hand over the dragon-blooded woman who worked at [[The Wandering Tit]], or Circle members die until she's found. Perce was the first example, killed before the party even returns to Underleaf in *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]* — the smoke and figures visible from the Burned Clearing at the start of that adventure.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** the practical logistics guy — kept caravans moving and deals fair, more organizer than orator.
- **Purpose:** Establishes that Ashra's ultimatum is real before the party's even met anyone — a death, not a threat, opens the adventure.
