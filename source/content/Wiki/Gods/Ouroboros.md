## (DM Only) The Dark of It
It died not through battle, but self-annihilation -- symbolic of its own dogma.

It still exists somewhere in both the [[Temporal Energy Plane]] and the [[Ordial Plane]], in a state of simultaneous life and death. Its body remains completely dead in the Temporal realm, endlessly decaying into dust. But its mathematical basis, the embodiment of recursion, exists within the Ordial.
