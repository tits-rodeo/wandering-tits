## Background
A quick, patched-leather goblin who knows every back path in and out of Underleaf. Attached to the resistance cell operating out of [[Brynn Thatcher]]'s brewery — sent to intercept the party on their return because she's fast and knows the terrain, not because anyone actually put her in charge of anything.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Morty (*Rick and Morty*) energy — nervy, stammering, visibly out of her depth, dragged into something bigger than she agreed to and complaining the whole way there while still, somehow, getting it done.
- **Voice:** Urgent, breathless, talks in a rush like she's worried she'll be caught mid-sentence.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** The one who intercepts the party before they can charge straight into town.
- **Home:** Attached to the resistance cell operating out of the Brewery.
- **Purpose:** Opening-scene contact for *[[Adventure - Liberating Underleaf|Liberating Underleaf]]*; redirects the party to Renn's Smithy rather than let them walk straight into a patrol.
