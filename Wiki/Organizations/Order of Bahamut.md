A militant order devoted to [[Bahamut]]'s teaching that dragons are not bound to serve Tiamat. 

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
Wary of [[Azhura]]'s draconic appearance at first, the order set that distrust aside once her faith proved sincere — they taught her Bahamut's doctrine, trained her as a paladin, and ultimately accepted her Oath of Vengeance. [[Sir Garrick]] served among them as her instructor.
