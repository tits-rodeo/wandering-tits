**Dearest Lord Helm,**

If You can hear me, give me a sign.

My days since signing on with a spelljammer crew to find my previous heroic aspects, Terrifying Blade, have not gone as intended. A massive, slug-like creature swallowed the ship whole. Upon awakening, we were in a cavernous space. The captain was slumped against a wall that wriggled and writhed in almost liquid black. Alive, yet most insidiously. He had been wounded in the crash, and his wound was yet festering, black as ink.

Pulling him along, determined to carry out Your will, my lord, I brought the captain through flesh-lined corridors and out a door.

After leaving that foul place, the captain and I encountered several figures: a half-dragon person in shining armor, an insect being, a tabaxi with a harp, and a hunched kenku in a mask with kanji script upon it.

The paladin, I swiftly learned, was Azhura. Understandably, she was suspicious of me until I mentioned I was a cleric. Sadly, we were not on the best terms. I did not want to return to the hell I and my companion had just escaped. The paladin tried to heal the captain, but his time was short for this world, and he became a horrific, macabre monstrosity. Sadly, I finished my former companion with a bolt of Your holy magic... or what I had remaining in my reserves. Your voice was silent. Absent.

With my new companions, we braved the maw of this evil place.

_There is a smudge on the page from wine spilled upon it._

The tabaxi, Trixie, had been acting strangely when I took a rather odd painting of a pale elf, I think. The unknown elf sought to resurrect some fallen god.

Sadly, the attempt to leave after confronting the undead god was not bloodless. Three of the tavern's patrons died; two consumed by that infernal place, and one well disintegrated by a god's beam magic.

I must confess my sin, oh Lord. In the effort to escape, I left Azhura to deal with the ichorous sinew creatures. My paladin companion yet lives, but the selfish need to mend my bond with Thee, Helm, blinded me to the needs of my —

_Several words are crossed out._

 — friends, kin, colleagues' safety, and my own self-respect.

I am still disquieted by Your absence, Helm. Have I not served You as You wished? Is my redemption from saving Patience from that fate but a blink in the eye of a hurricane? I long to hear Your guidance, oh Lord, but I will make my own way if You requirest.

Yours in service and watch,

**Quinsley Alvinir**
