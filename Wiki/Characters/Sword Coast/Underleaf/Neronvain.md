Exiled second son of [[Melandrach|King Melandrach]] of the [[Misty Forest]], cast out by his own father and long believed dead. He rose through the [[Cult of the Dragon]] to become its Green Wyrmspeaker, allied with the green dragon [[Chuth]]. Occupied [[Underleaf|Wreathwood]] decades before either of those things were true, leading the cult cell known as **the Verdant Claw** in his first real test of what he could take and hold (see *[[The Underfoot]]*).

## (DM Only) Personality
Where Melandrach is circumspect, cautious, arguing for patience even when it costs him, Neronvain (like his half-brother Alagarthas) always rushed headlong into his own pursuits. He isn't a mindless brute, though: he fights and negotiates as a spellcaster first, leaning on illusion and charm rather than brute force, and he's willing to bargain with an enemy when it's useful rather than simply kill them. 

During the Altand raid, the warden Galin begged for his life mid-battle after watching his wife die. Neronvain didn't just spare him. He struck a deal, sparing Galin and leaving his village intact in exchange for ongoing intelligence on which settlement the cult should raid next, and held to that specific bargain even as he burned every other village Galin fed him. Transactional, patient, and willing to keep a promise to one person while breaking the world around them.

A journal recovered from his private chambers refers to Melandrach repeatedly, and only ever as "my poor father." Not hatred exactly, something closer to contempt tangled up with grief. He decorated those same chambers with looted art and tapestries taken from the elves of the Misty Forest, trophies from his own people.

## (DM Only) After Wreathwood
Wreathwood was Neronvain's proving ground, not his legacy. He carried what he learned there into the wider Cult of the Dragon, eventually earning the Green Dragon Mask and a seat among Severin's inner circle as Wyrmspeaker, alongside Chuth. In that role he turned on his own kin, leading raids against elven settlements throughout the Misty Forest (including the destruction of Altand) to gather tribute toward the Mask of the Dragon Queen, all while his father Melandrach believed him long dead.

A party of adventurers eventually tracked him and Chuth to their stronghold deep in the Misty Forest and took him alive. Chuth broke off and fled once the fight turned, escaping to report the loss to Severin at the Well of Dragons rather than die defending the stronghold. Neronvain himself was handed over to the Council of Waterdeep and, by extension, to Melandrach: the father who exiled him for what he was, now forced to reckon with what he'd since become. Melandrach's public reaction was shame and rage rather than relief. What became of Neronvain after that reckoning is still unresolved, a loose thread rather than a closed one, and so is Chuth, last known to still be at large.
