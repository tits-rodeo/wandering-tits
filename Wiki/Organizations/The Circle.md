The governing body of [[Underleaf]]. The town has no lord or council in the traditional sense. Disputes and decisions go before seven townsfolk, chosen by lot every season, with the understanding that anyone might be asked to serve. It's slow and informal, but it keeps any one faction, or any one grudge, from taking hold of the town for long.

## The Crimson Flame Occupation
When [[Ashra]] of the [[Crimson Flame]] seized Underleaf, she took the Circle hostage. Her terms: hand over the dragon-blooded woman who worked at [[The Wandering Tit]], or one member of the Circle burns each day until the town complies. [[Perce Hollin]] was the first to die. [[Vantha Emberlight]], next in line, was freed from the administrative center by the party before her execution.

## Seats at the Time of the Occupation
1. **[[Old Maud]]:** Fixture of The Wandering Tit, founding member of [[The Underfoot]]. Died before the occupation.
2. **[[Tok Tok]]**: Owner of [[The Wandering Tit]]
3. **[[Oswin Marrow]]:** Grocer
4. **[[Brynn Thatcher]]:** Brewer
5. **[[Fen|Wrinklefenderly "Fen" Delimimus]]:** Local abjurer wizard
6. **[[Perce Hollin]]:** Human caravan master, handled Trade Way logistics. Executed by Ashra.
7. **[[Vantha Emberlight]]:** Dragonborn ex-soldier, the Circle's lone defense-minded voice. Rescued.
