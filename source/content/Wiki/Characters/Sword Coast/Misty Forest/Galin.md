## Background
A warden of the forest and, since the raid, the de facto leader of [[Altand]]. A slim, nervous-looking elf, weighed down by the deaths of more than a hundred villagers, including his own wife. He keeps a pet raven and is known to slip away from the village most nights.

## (DM Only) The Truth
Galin came face to face with [[Neronvain]] mid-battle after watching his wife die, and begged for his life. Neronvain agreed to spare him and leave Altand standing in exchange for ongoing intelligence on which elven settlements the cult should raid next. Galin has kept that oath ever since, sneaking out most nights (accompanied by his raven) to report to Neronvain at a prearranged meeting spot in the woods, and has told no one in Altand the truth. He's wracked with guilt over it.

## (DM Only) Roleplay Notes
- Publicly, he claims not to have seen the dragon rider at all and dismisses others' accounts as fear and confusion.
- A DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals he's withholding something. Pressed, he admits he watched the dragon kill his wife and blames himself for failing to save her. A follow-up DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals even that isn't the whole truth, but he takes umbrage at further questioning.
- If confronted with real evidence (spellcraft, subterfuge, or having followed him to his meeting spot), he goes on the offensive, publicly accusing the party of working with the cult to undermine Altand's survivors. He refuses to submit to *zone of truth* or similar magic.
- Intimidation doesn't work on him. Commiseration does: offered sympathy for his wife's death rather than accusation, he eventually breaks down and confesses.
