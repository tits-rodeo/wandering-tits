*A human fighter with wild reddish-orange hair swept up in a high ponytail. An eyepatch covers one eye. His face is alive with an unhinged grin. He moves with the ease of someone who has fought a thousand times before, reckless and eager. A wooden peg leg replaces his right leg below the knee, yet he moves with surprising grace despite it. His back is always bristling with weapons of every kind—swords, spears, hammers, bows. Many appear from nowhere when he reaches for them, materialized by means unknown.*

## Background

Reckless. Enthusiastic. All bluster and show, though his combat experience is undeniable. He approaches fights like they're entertainment, shouting out attack names in dramatic fashion. Underneath the chaos, he's a skilled fighter who respects competence. Those who impress him in combat earn his respect—and possibly his companionship.

Corvus works as as a "minder" (security) for [[Damroodleboss Delimimus]]'s warehouse in Sigil. How he acquired his missing eye and leg remains unclear. He is reckless and enthusiastic in combat, cycling through different weapons with abandon.
