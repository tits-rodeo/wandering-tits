**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Rare)_ 
- You may take a Bonus Action to cause your clothes/armor to assume the appearance of a normal set of clothing or armor. You decide what it looks like -- including color, style, and accessories -- but any armor retains its normal bulk and weight. The illusory appearance lasts until you use this property again or doff the clothes/armor.

## (DM Only) Origin
Given to [[Azhura]] by a dying priest during a [[Crimson Flame]] raid on a monastery of Bahamut roughly six years ago — she had broken from the raiding party to try to protect the priests, and failed to save this one, but he pressed the amulet into her hands before he died. It became her first real connection to Bahamut's faith, a source of comfort and quiet strength through her curse, exile, and everything after. She still doesn't know whether it was ever anything more than a keepsake, or whether it holds power of its own.

She's never learned the priest's name, or why the amulet alone survived the raid intact. Whether Bahamut ever actually speaks through it, or whether what she takes for guidance is only visions, dreams, or half-imagined signs during moments of extreme distress, she can't say for certain — nor whether it might yet awaken further as she grows stronger.
