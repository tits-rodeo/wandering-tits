North of Waterdeep, and home to the concealed domain of the [[Crimson Flame]]. Waterdeep's rulers and informed regional elites know Skaerth's clan exists somewhere in these peaks, but they badly underestimate its true scope — hidden valleys, underground routes, false maps, and mortal proxies keep the kingdom's real hierarchy, population, and purpose out of sight. Most who pass through only ever notice scattered mines, isolated estates, cult activity, or the occasional monster, and describe it as a dragon clan or regional legend rather than a functioning state.
