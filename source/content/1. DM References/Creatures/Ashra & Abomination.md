*[[Ashra]], in dragon form, locked in an aerial grapple with the veiled skull of the Moander abomination, wrapped in a churning mass of vines and tentacles. The party isn't the target, just in the way.*

>[!statblocks]
># Ashra & Abomination
>**Type:** Gargantuan Aberration
>**Alignment:** Unaligned
>######
> **CR:** 10 (5,900 XP)
> **AC:** 16
> **HP:** 420 (40d20)
> **Speed:** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>######
> **STR** 27 (+8) **DEX** 10 (+0) **CON** 25 (+7)
> **INT** 16 (+3) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 16 (+3)
>######
> **Condition Immunities:** Grappled, Prone, Restrained
> **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft., Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
> **Languages:** —
> **Proficiency Bonus:** +4
>###### Traits
> - **Two Pools.** Ashra 170 HP, Abomination 250 HP. A hit strikes a random pool (d2). If the attack beats AC by 3+, the attacker chooses. Area effects and saves hit both pools.
> - **Exhausted Grapple.** Dexterity saves at disadvantage.
> - **Rotted Core.** Abomination pool takes double fire and radiant damage. Ashra is immune to fire.
> - **Trailing Tendrils.** While airborne (about 30 ft. up), melee attacks only reach the Abomination pool.
> - **Locked Together.** Immune to grappled, prone, and restrained except from the siege net. Other conditions affect Ashra only, and she deals no Struggle damage while affected.
> - **Infection.** If Ashra drops to 0 while the Abomination has 63+ HP, it takes her corpse as its new host.
> - **Netted.** Crashes to the ground: each creature within 15 ft. of the landing makes a DC 15 Dex save, taking 10 (3d6) bludgeoning on a failure. Each pool takes 14 (4d6). While netted: AC 14, no flying, Trailing Tendrils ends.
>###### Actions
> - **Struggle.** Each half rolls a d4 and deals that damage to the other.
> **Ashra → Abomination:** (1) Rend, 14 (1d10+8) slashing + 10 (2d4, doubled) fire. (2) Bite and Tear, 19 (2d10+8) piercing. (3) Claw Rake, 15 (2d6+8) slashing. (4) Point-blank Breath, 42 (6d6, doubled) fire, 1/fight, then use Rend.
> **Abomination → Ashra:** (1) Rotting Tentacles, 15 (3d6+5) bludgeoning + 7 (2d6) necrotic. (2) Point-blank Eye Beam, 28 (8d6) necrotic. (3) Constrict, 18 (4d8) bludgeoning, halve Ashra's next Struggle. (4) Spore Injection, 10 (3d6) poison + 7 (2d6) necrotic.
> - **Misfire (x2).** Roll a d10 twice.
> **1-3, Deflected Breath.** 30-ft cone toward a random PC. DC 15 Dex, 21 (6d6) fire, half on success.
> **4, Flung Tentacle.** +7 vs a random creature within 60 ft. 13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning, grappled (escape DC 14), hauled 15 ft. up.
> **5, Wing Downdraft.** 20-ft radius below. DC 15 Str or 7 (2d6) bludgeoning, pushed 15 ft., prone.
> **6, Stray Eye Beam.** 60-ft line, random direction. DC 15 Dex, 18 (4d8) necrotic, half on success.
> **7, Spore Burst.** 20-ft sphere. DC 14 Con or 10 (3d6) poison and poisoned until end of next turn. Corpses inside rise as vine zombies next round.
> **8, Falling Debris.** 10-ft square. DC 14 Dex, 14 (4d6) bludgeoning, half on success. Area becomes difficult terrain, burning.
> **9, Sloughed Growth.** 1d2 vine blights drop from the mass.
> **10, Scorching Ray.** As the spell. Three rays, each at a random creature within 120 ft. +9 to hit, 7 (2d6) fire each.

## Notes
- **Net:** siege weapon at the west edge of town, brought by soldiers on Ashra's order.
- **Shield of Tobias:** negates Deflected Breath.
- **Dragon Slayer weapons:** bonus damage only on hits to Ashra's pool.
- **Etching solution:** only if Ashra survives and stays hostile.
- **Infected escape:** ad-hoc, using Ashra's infected dragon form.
