**Lord Helm,**

Holy is Thy name, and the works You direct on the material plane; I hope to prove worthy in carrying them out.

Returning to Mount Celestia, much has changed in the hours since my last journal entry. After we arrived, we met with a great wizard in Underleaf. We also met with resistance fighters in the town, who happened to be members of the ruling circle.

At a rather harrowing interaction with the town elder, an elderly tiefling who clutched a ledger like a safety item, I could smell the expensive parchment and the specific mix of the ink. I learned that one of the tavern patrons who died was the elder's adoptive son. Predictably, he had difficult words for me: 

***Why did I survive when his son had not?***

I attempted, as courteously and respectfully as I could, to say that I wished our positions were reversed; that he was here, not me. It gave us a clear understanding of how to proceed.

My companions and I went to collect a powerful focusing item: a shield with electrum lining. The metal was worn smooth in places, rough in others, like a path walked too many times. It did not gleam. It simply was. This speaks to what the elder had mentioned. A green dragon had previously oppressed the people of the town.

However, when we found the shield, we also discovered a helmet and pauldron belonging to a previous warrior, an Aasimar devoted to You, Lord Helm. The pauldron bore tally marks, as if tracking chances and avoiding death.

*A small ring stains the corner of the page, as if she set her cup down without looking.*

I am unnerved by this previous Aasimar hero. Their story and adventures are eerily familiar to me. From the defense I made on behalf of Bethany —

*A smear of ink crosses the word "monsters" as she started to write something else, then stopped.*

— I did not personally know this hero. My memories of my past life, prior to the ascension, remain absent. Yet there are a number of overlaps between this hero and me.

After this town has been freed, I believe I must pursue this lead and find out more about the work of this previous hero. Perhaps it will reveal something of my past and help me remember the life and work I had before.

I decided to put the helmet on my head. I saw a vision, surreal and present. It was from the previous attack. I saw it from their perspective, and I saw an elf fighting on behalf of the green dragon. During this horrifying encounter, my party and I fought a dark apparition of this monster. The evil elf was dealt with.

One thing continues to linger with me, even after returning to see the elder. I saw him there among the children, among those who feared the intruders and oppressors. We did not speak again, but he recognized the helmet I now wear.

Up until that point, I had no personal investment in this town or in this fight. But I know, Helm, that personal investment is irrelevant. What matters is doing what is right and protecting the innocent. These people have protected themselves. And if I wish to make a life for myself here, I must not become nothing more than a wanderer... a nomad. That life led me onto that spelljammer ship, where I encountered that horrifying slug-like thing. And yet still, I am no closer to finding its warhammer of power.

But there is more. Our half-dragon paladin seems to have a troubling compulsion, a need to amass gold, jewels, and treasure of any kind. To make a hoard! I discovered this when I encountered a bag of gold and a health potion. [[Azhura]] acted as if her life depended on that gold. My mind initially believed she was afraid that I would hoard the gold myself. But we both know, O Lord, that I pay my own way and never intend to be a burden upon my comrades.

*There is a rough patch on the parchment, as if Quinsley gripped the page too tightly.*

I shall endeavor to keep my correspondence consistent.

Yours in service and devotion,

**Quinsley**

*A series of glyphs scorched into the page—her divine or true name.*

**Lord Helm,**

I am yours in action, purpose, and dedication.

Much has happened since I last wrote about the party. I found [[Old Maud]]'s weapon cache, and some of us took weapons for dragon slaying.

*A blot of ink sits where Quinsley seems to have corrected her own writing, the quill bleeding into the page.*

After arranging for the weapons to be distributed, we moved on to try and free [[Vantha Emberlight|a dragonborn member]] of [[The Circle|the circle]], their more security-conscious constituent. The rescue involved a series of fights against [[Crimson Flame|dragon cultist]] troops. I write this guarding an unconscious cultist, but I hear [[Azhura]] crying; something to do with that bitch of a dragon sister of hers, [[Ashra]]. I will have firm and comforting words for our paladin companion. Comfort and compassion are teachings I learned from You, O Lord.

We are at a crossroads. Events will likely escalate, but we have some ideas for tipping the balance. Some of the villagers mentioned a [[Mireille Duskweave|fortune teller]]...

*Some writing in Celestial follows, roughly translating to "I hate prophecies. They got me back down here in the first place."*

...and a noble act to rescue [[Tobin]]'s family from cultist thugs. It is what is right to save Tobin's family, and hopefully make up for the guilt I have for [[Garrek|the half-orc]]'s death.

*More scorch marks and a gouge in the parchment, as if Quinsley is trying to contain recrimination or guilt.*

I will endeavor to write again soon, Lord Helm, as You deserve to know of the goings-on in Thy cleric's work.

As Thy humble servant in watch,

**Quinsley Alvenir**

*Her angelic name, once again painstakingly written, but with clear signs of less care in the calligraphy.*
