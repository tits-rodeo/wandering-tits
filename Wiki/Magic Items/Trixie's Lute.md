**Magic Item** 
_(Minor, Rare)_ 
- While you are playing this musical instrument, you can take a Magic action to have your lute generate the sound of any number of different accompanying instruments. You can only generate the sounds of instruments you are proficient in (or that are mostly similar). 
- As long as you play for at least 1 minute, the quality of your enhanced music can have a profound affect on the people who hear it: Make a Performance check against DC 5. If successful, you have advantage on all Charisma checks directed at one creature of your choice who heard the performance for 1 minute. For every 5 points by which your Performance check exceeded DC 5, you are able to affect one additional target (eg: If you roll a total of 15 on your Performance check, you may affect up to 3 different targets who heard your performance).
