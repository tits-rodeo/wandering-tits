Patriarch of the [[Crimson Flame]], Inquisitor of [[Tiamat]], an ancient red wyrm whose tyranny has dominated the region for centuries. It is a fairly vast territory where some of his offspring reside and are treated as his property.

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
Hatched roughly a millennium ago, Skaerth spent centuries serving in dragon legions under gith command before turning that wealth and experience toward carving out his own territory along the [[Sword Coast]], eventually settling his lair in the [[Sword Mountains]]. He took an early interest in [[Sammaster]]'s [[Cult of the Dragon]], but rejected its dracolich doctrine outright from the start — Skaerth is a practical zealot of Tiamat who has always believed living dragons, not undead ones, should rule in her name. He founded the Crimson Flame not long after Sammaster's own movement collapsed, and spent the following centuries as a heterodox voice on the fringes of the wider Cult, whose mainstream doctrine remained fixated on dracolich apotheosis and answered to no single deity. Only in recent decades, with a reformist Cult leader turning much of the organization toward Tiamat and living draconic rule, has Skaerth's long-held position finally found an echo in the wider Cult — for the first time in five centuries, his heresy looks like orthodoxy.

His claim to the region rests on a single decisive victory: an elder black dragon, older than Skaerth himself, once tried to encroach on his growing territory. Skaerth fought alongside his own offspring while the rival fought alone, and won. He used that victory to compel every other dragon in and around the Sword Mountains to submit to him — those who refused were driven out or killed.

He regards his own children as property, weapons, and prized pieces of his hoard. When one turns apostate or rebellious — as [[Azhura]] did — his preference is always to reclaim and break them back into obedience rather than destroy them; killing his own offspring is a last resort, not a first instinct.

- Attacked a secluded monastery dedicated to [[Bahamut]].
