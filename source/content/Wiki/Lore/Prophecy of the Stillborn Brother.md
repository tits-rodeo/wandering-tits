Six hands reached for heaven's crown,
The loom it screamed, the loom came down.
One thread lived. One thread cried.
Something else was born — and died.

Born and died, born and died.
The stillborn brother... forever dies.

The last thread keeps the gate of ends,
Counts the debts and mourns his kin.
The stillborn brother waits up high.
The last thread's secret, he knows why.

Born and died, born and died
Could it be, the stillborn never died?

The tree that holds the sky takes wound.
The wound is crawling, the wound is soon.
The garden blackens, root to crown,
And grief goes up where light went down.

Grief calls hunger. Hunger waits.
Grief calls hunger. Hunger wakes.

It drinks the scream the garden made,
It rides the scream, black flame, unmade.
Toward the cradle, and dragon kin,
The stillborn brother comes for them!

Grief calls hunger. Hunger takes.
Grief gives hunger what it makes.

Gold scales, red scales: Siblings doomed.
They forget their war, it ends them too.
No love, no peace; just one truth shared:
Nothing wins when nothing's spared.

Born and died, born and died,
The stillborn brother cares not why.

Six legs, one debt, a distant crown.
The blades that broke her, melted down.
Go home before the stillborn flies!
No fang but hers was built its size.

Born and died, born and died,
Here it comes to kiss the sky!

Takes the vessel, sups its dreams,
Drinks the hope and hate it needs.
Sends her wandering through the seams
Of heaven, earth, and hell, and...?

Born and died, born and died.
Born and died, born and died.
Born and died, born and died.
The stillborn brother's vengeance... is nigh!
