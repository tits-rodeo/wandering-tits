A cleric at [[Bahamut]]'s monastery alongside [[Sir Garrick]]. She and Garrick were the ones who found and healed [[Azhura]] after she crash-landed near the temple five years ago, and were among the few at the monastery who knew what Azhura truly was. Died alongside Garrick and nearly everyone else when the monastery was burned down by the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and agents of the [[Crimson Flame]].
