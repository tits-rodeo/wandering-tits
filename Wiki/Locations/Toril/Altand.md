A wood elf settlement in the [[Misty Forest]], built on two levels: a few ground structures around an ancient monument to an elven ranger, with residences and the village temple built into the upper boughs and linked by rope bridges and pulley platforms. One of the villages raided by [[Neronvain]] and [[Chuth]] during the cult's push for tribute toward the Mask of the Dragon Queen, and the only one to survive relatively intact.

## History
Of Altand's three hundred residents, more than half survived the raid, a stark contrast to the other settlements the dragon hunted to the last adult and child. The village's priest died trying to fight the dragon off, swallowed whole, and many elves in the upper boughs died to poison breath or fell as the walkways collapsed under the weight of panic. Then, uncharacteristically, the attack broke off early. The cultists retreated without looting anything, a departure from every other raid, which were fought for treasure.

The reason surfaced later: the village's warden, [[Galin]], had come face to face with Neronvain mid-battle after watching his wife die, and struck a bargain rather than dying alongside her. Neronvain agreed to spare Galin and leave Altand standing in exchange for ongoing intelligence on which settlements the cult should hit next. Galin kept that oath afterward, sneaking out most nights to report to Neronvain, and has said nothing of it to his own people. With the priest dead, he's since become Altand's de facto leader, credited with rallying the survivors, unaware anyone might connect his composure to the truth.
