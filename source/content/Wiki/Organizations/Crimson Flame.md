One of the most feared red dragon clans of the Sword Mountains — in truth less a clan than a concealed feudal kingdom, built by [[Skaerth]] to replace the usual isolation of chromatic dragons with something durable enough to dominate the region and serve [[Tiamat]]. Waterdeep's rulers and informed regional elites know the Crimson Flame exists, but they badly underestimate its true territory, population, and purpose; most outsiders write off its mines, estates, cult cells, and monster sightings as unconnected local trouble rather than a single organized state.

## (DM Only) Hierarchy
The clan runs on a feudal structure layered directly onto draconic pride:
- **Sovereign** — Skaerth alone. Final word on law, land, rank, and religious legitimacy. Treats every grant of power as revocable.
- **High nobility** — reserved exclusively for Skaerth's acknowledged direct children. They administer major holdings and command large forces, but remain his property regardless of rank.
- **Middle nobility** — true chromatic dragons who have submitted to Skaerth's authority in exchange for keeping their own territory and forces.
- **Lower nobility** — half-dragons, dragonborn, and exceptional mortals (including Cult of the Dragon leaders and Tiamatite clergy) granted baronial or knightly rank through draconic patronage.
- **Subjects and serfs** — aligned commoners beneath any rank, and non-aligned mortal populations kept under direct, largely unprotected control.
- **Livestock and war stock** — drakes, wyverns, and animalistic monsters, with no standing of their own.

## (DM Only) Economy and war
Mines, forges, and taxation from lower nobles form the clan's productive base, but Skaerth deliberately caps tribute so the kingdom always stays ready for war. Most dragon nobles build their own personal wealth through raids and dungeon-delving rather than domestic extraction — plunder that channels draconic aggression outward instead of into infighting. Skaerth prefers intimidation, proxies, and targeted raids to full-scale war, which is costly and rarely as profitable.

## (DM Only) Faith and the Cult of the Dragon
Skaerth is a practical zealot of Tiamat, and maintains a small outpost of the [[Church of Tiamat]] within his territory — far from the church's true seats of power in Unther and Chessenta, but enough to supply the clan with doctrine, ritual, and a political intermediary or two. He valued the original [[Cult of the Dragon]] for its mortal network but rejected its dracolich obsession from the start, a heterodox position that got him nowhere with the wider Cult for centuries; its mainstream doctrine remained fixated on undeath and answered to no single deity. Only recently, as a reformist Cult leader turned much of the organization toward Tiamat and living draconic rule, has Skaerth found unexpected validation — and Crimson Flame agents now quietly work to draw sympathetic cells further into his orbit while that rare alignment lasts. [[Lord Halvern]] is one such asset.

## (DM Only) The founding victory
At some point after all his acknowledged children had hatched, an elder black dragon far older than Skaerth tried to encroach on his territory. Skaerth fought beside his own offspring while the rival fought alone, and won — proof, in his eyes, that unified dragons could overcome even a stronger solitary one. He used the victory to force every other dragon near the Sword Mountains to submit; those who refused were killed or driven out.

## (DM Only) Reach beyond the mountains
The clan projects power across the Sword Coast through Cult cells, subordinate nobles, merchants, and small operational teams, often passing orders through several intermediaries so it's unclear whether a given mission traces back to Skaerth, one of his children, or a local commander. Capture operations favor manufacturing a public crisis first — fire, danger to bystanders, visible cruelty — to divide defenders and terrorize survivors, matching red dragons' taste for fear and domination over quiet efficiency.
