## Ideas
- Feywild's weird time properties could imply deeper connection to the Temporal Energy Plane. Then again, there's a fair number of demiplanes with weird time mechanics.
- Planar Geometries:
	- All the inner, outer, transitive, etc. planes exist according to the rules of Euclidean geometry.
	- [[The Far Realm]] exists according the rules of a non-Euclidean geometry, hyperbolic geometry.
	- The [[Temporal Energy Plane]] exists according to the rules of another non-Euclidean geometry, elliptic geometry.
	- The "planes" refer to these geometric concepts.
- Crossover Event (Planar Pathfinder + Wandering Tits)
	- Planar Pathfinders end up in Astral Plane. Eaten by the same Astral Dreadnought as the Wandering Tits escaped into in their first adventure
- Black obelisks
	- Original [[Spellweaver]] obelisks unaccounted for. Could place one in Faerun (in [[The Underfoot]] tunnels), and five more in locations across the multiverse for the Planar Pathfinders to find.
	- The [[Underleaf]] obelisk could be used by the party as a sort of waypoint system, allowing them to teleport to any known location for free once per day, and also utilize it as a teleportation circle. Will need to create Magic Item stats for this, along with other Artifact traits. Perhaps also make it so only Mantis, with the help of her amulet, can tap into this power (for now, arcane practicioners of suitable level also could eventually).
	- The obelisk in [[The Underfoot]] tunnels may also allow a sort of time travel, but only spiritually, to "past incarnations" bearing the traveler's "soul signature." Requires possessing something that existed around the target era to anchor the trip. Party should eventually obtain relics from the Days of Thunder, letting each individual character time travel to a point in each Creator Race's history (saruukh, batrachi, aearee, leshay, humans).
	- [[Jergal]] may have finally located all but one of the gems (memory fragments) for the Code of Reversion (Wandering Tits could locate the final one). Then Jergal just needs to locate the other five obelisks, and could then cast the spell using them.
	- Code of Reversion would likely be used right after [[Atropus]] destroys Toril, to send the party (or parties, if we do a crossover adventure) to the early multiverse, in the time of the [[Spellweaver]]s, and try to find a solution to Atropus there.
- Aboleths
	- Planar Pathfinders will probably want to locate one
	- Aboleths being some of the oldest creatures in existence, from a time before gods -- they may have existed even in the rumored period during which the multiverse may have been a single existence. In this case, the stygian aboleth may have a vested interest in not only the Code of Reversion, but also to seeing the ascension ritual of the Spellweavers succeed in order to return the universe to that state and see that aboleths rekindle their old empire. If this was the original purpose of the ritual, then ascension was either only intended as a side effect or was an accident.

## Atropus
- What actually happened when Atropus arrived at Glyth (~-11,000 DR) is still open. Drawn there by mass death from gith purges against illithid survivors, per [[Atropus]]. Could be what finishes off the remnant population and forces the flight to Oryndoll, rather than Atropus just arriving to an empty world.
- The Temporal Energy Plane above could be the literal mechanism behind Atropus's "stretches backward and forward in time" origin (tied to the First Sundering) rather than leaving it abstract. Worth reconciling the two. Could be a good hook to draw Planar Pathfinders character into the Wandering Tits campaign.
- H'Catha and Garden still need to be worked out as the route Atropus takes toward Toril in the modern day. Started at H'Catha (beholder factional war, orog pirates aboard the Scavenger, possible Glyth tie-in later), ending at Garden (already named in the Stillborn Brother prophecy: "the garden blackens, root to crown"), then slingshotting toward Toril.
- Possible later payoff: Atropus finally does make the old Elder Evils Glyth-destruction lore real, in the modern day rather than the ancient past.
- Whether other crystal spheres/settings got their own Atropus-adjacent incident from the same Disjunction backlash (per the Positive/Negative Energy draw being multiversal) stays open. Could stay implied/off-screen, or become a real hook later.

- Monument of the Ancients, Sorcerer's Isle, Thunderholme: Three secondary spellweaver nodes?
- Dungeon #170 ("Monument of Ancients") - May have more spellweaver lore
- Spellweavers connect to Jergal's mortal self (i.e., he was a mortal spellweaver) and his divine self; thri-kreen connect only to his divine self, no mortal-life tie
- Research Eril more, it seems to be critical to Jergal/Spellweaver lore. Pretty sure this is the node Jergal originated from?
- Besides Jergal, might be five other spellweavers that ascended in other Material worlds. Dark Sun would have one for sure, if so. Probably Oerth as well.
- Vecna retconning spellweaver existence might be worth accepting. Jergal would obviously have survived, and having spellweavers as a dead/forgotten species would be far more mysterious/spooky. Don't ever show the scary thing!
	- How would he do this without gathering the gems/Code of Reversion, though? Even as a former god of secrets, this does feel like a bit of a stretch if nailed down that the Code is _required_ in addition to the Obelisks.

## Factions / Organizations
- Fractals, or "The Shrinkers": Believe all plane are actually one, an "overplane", and each plane is actually an infinitesimally small location within the overplane. Their proof is a portal that leads back into Sigil, but shrinks those who pass through it (this isn't at all  normal portal behavior).
