## Background
A traveling Aasimar of [[Helm]]'s order, passing through [[Underleaf|Wreathwood]] during its occupation by [[Neronvain]]'s Verdant Claw. He threw in with [[The Underfoot]] rather than move on, and died fighting alongside them in the final push that drove the cult out. He's buried in what's now Underleaf's Cemetery, one grave among the Underfoot's other dead, unmarked and unremarked. His cursed ceremonial helm survived him, lost somewhere in the tunnels the Underfoot dug beneath the town (see *[[The Underfoot]]*) until [[Quinsley]] finds it generations later.

## (DM Only) Personality (Roleplay Notes)
- **Trope / Character Reference:** Gallows-humor battlefield medic energy — devout but irreverent, cracks dark jokes mid-fight, copes with what vigilance actually costs by refusing to treat any of it solemnly.
- **Voice:** Rapid, restless chatter that never quite stops, even mid-combat. Narrates his own wounds and field stitches like a running bit, using the noise to keep himself and everyone nearby moving instead of freezing.
- **Memorable Tidbit:** Kept a running tally scratched into his pauldron of every wound he'd survived, not for vanity, more like keeping score against death itself. The pauldron had obviously been repaired many times over.
- **Appearance:** Battle-worn well before Wreathwood. Close-cropped grey-white hair, a knot of old scars across one brow, celestial features weathered rather than serene.
- **Home:** Unknown. He never spoke of it, and it doesn't become clear in the flashback either — deliberately left a mystery, a thread to pull later.
- **Purpose:** Why he was in the Misty Forest at all was unclear even to the Underfoot at the time. He dropped hints mid-fight that he was looking for someone, or something, out there, and never got to finish saying what.
