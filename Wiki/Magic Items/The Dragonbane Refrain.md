**Magic Item**
_(Song, learned)_
- **Bonus Action** to start or sustain, once per round. While playing, you and everyone within 60 ft. have advantage on saving throws against dragons' abilities and spells.
- Granting Bardic Inspiration is folded into the same Bonus Action at no extra cost.

## (DM Only) Origin
Learned by [[Trixie]] from the hand-copied *Compleat Dragon-Hunter* recovered from Old Maud's Hut, an old dragon-hunting manual reproduced at Candlekeep by a member of the original Underfoot resistance. Whether the song stops if Trixie is Silenced, Incapacitated, or forced by an effect like Command's Halt or Drop is a live DM call, not a hard rule.
