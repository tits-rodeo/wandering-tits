## An Overview
- Easy mode access to arcane energy
	- Use of is called "the Art"
	- Ability to use called "the Gift" -- raw talent
- One of numerous means of access to arcane energy. Others include:
	- Table magic
	- True name magic
- Mystra IS the Weave
- "Ghosts" can exist in the Weave for an individual after death
- Weave Anchors
	- Mortal -- Power is permanent, Mystra can't remove once bestowed
	- Vested from Mystra's own power -- she becomes lesser when vested to someone
- 

## The Shadow Weave
- Spaces in between the Weave
- Relies on the Weave, since it's basically a reflection (shadow) of it
- Leeches energy from the Weave

## Spellfire
