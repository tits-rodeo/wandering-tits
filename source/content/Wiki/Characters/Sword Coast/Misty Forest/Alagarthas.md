># Alagarthas
>###### Stats
>Type|Stat|

>**Race**|Elf|

Prince of the [[Misty Forest]], son of [[Melandrach|King Melandrach]] and half-brother of [[Neronvain]] (different mothers). Like Neronvain, he always rushed headlong into his own pursuits, in contrast to his father's caution.

## History
When Melandrach declared the Misty Forest's dragon raids ended, Alagarthas disagreed, and used the Emerald Enclave to keep digging for the source of the attacks. Working alongside Delaan Winterhound, he learned that an elf appeared to be commanding the green dragon responsible, and grew convinced further attacks were coming, that the dragon's master might be one of the Cult of the Dragon's wyrmspeakers. What neither he nor Delaan realized is that the wyrmspeaker in question was his own half-brother.
