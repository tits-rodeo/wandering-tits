[[Azhura]]'s mother and [[Valraxis]]'s foster mother. A red dragon who had once been raised among mortals in an attempt to see whether one of her kind could grow up free of the hatred and cruelty of traditional red-dragon culture — it worked, for a time.

[[Skaerth]] captured her, killed everyone she loved in the process, and kept her chained within his lair for centuries. The isolation and torment eventually broke her sanity, but never quite her capacity for gentleness: she became a genuine foster mother to Valraxis and, decades later, raised Azhura with real compassion, passing on as much of her mortal-taught values as she could manage from inside a cage.

When Skaerth realized Azhura was growing into something other than the weapon he wanted, Cinder opposed him outright. She entrusted Azhura to Valraxis before the final confrontation and died as a result of it, roughly forty years into Azhura's life.

Azhura's earliest memories are of Cinder's prison chamber.
